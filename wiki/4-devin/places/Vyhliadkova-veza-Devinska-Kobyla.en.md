# Lookout Tower on Devínska Kobyla (Vyhliadková veža na Devínskej Kobyle)

A white steel lookout tower on Devínska Kobyla (514 m), the highest point of Bratislava, opened in 2020. The design by the studio Architekti Šebo Lichý (Tomáš Šebo, Igor Lichý and team) is built on an unusual form: a triangular base expands parametrically upward, so the tower seems to open toward the sky — a silhouette at once iconic and unobtrusive amid the protected meadows. At 21.27 m tall the structure weighs a mere 35 tonnes; exactly 112 steps lead to the top platform, which commands a panorama of three countries at once — Slovakia, Austria and Czechia.

![Lookout Tower on Devínska Kobyla (Vyhliadková veža na Devínskej Kobyle)](/assets/vyhliadkova-veza-devinska-kobyla.jpg)

The tower was built by the borough of Devínska Nová Ves with financial participation of the developer ITB Development. The small structure quickly gathered a notable collection of recognition: the BigSEE Architecture Award (2021), shortlists of Slovakia's CE ZA AR, the Dušan Jurkovič Prize and the Piranesi Exhibition, and in summer 2025 the renowned architecture portal ArchDaily ranked it among the 25 most remarkable observation towers in the world — a rare international success for Slovak architecture.

Access is on foot along trails through the meadows and woods of the Devínska Kobyla national nature reserve: up from Devín, from Devínska Nová Ves (past the [[Sandberg|Sandberg]] sand outcrop) or from Dúbravka; near the summit the path also passes an abandoned Cold War missile base. Cadastrally the summit belongs to Devín, though the tower was built by neighbouring Devínska Nová Ves — on the map it holds the boundary between the two boroughs.
