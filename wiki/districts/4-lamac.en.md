# Lamač (Bratislava IV)

Lamač is one of Bratislava's smallest boroughs, in the city's north-west in a saddle of the Little Carpathians (Bratislava IV okres). Before joining the city it was a separate village with a winegrowing and stone-cutting past — the very name is derived from quarrying (lámať, "to break"). The old core — Starý Lamač with its village layout — can still be read around Vrančovičova street; post-war quarters and the transport corridors towards Záhorie have grown around it.

The borough's landmark is the [[Kostol-svatej-Margity|Church of St Margaret]] (1947–1951), one of the last works of [[Harminc-Milan-Michal|Milan Michal Harminc]], which replaced a cramped 16th-century church.

## Coat of Arms

![Coat of arms of Lamač](/assets/erb-4-lamac.png)

In a blue field, St Margaret of Antioch, crowned and in a red cloak, with a golden cross and palm branch, trampling a golden dragon. She is the patroness of the Lamač parish — the [[Kostol-svatej-Margity|Church of St Margaret]] is dedicated to her too.
