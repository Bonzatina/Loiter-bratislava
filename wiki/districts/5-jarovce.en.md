# Jarovce (Bratislava V)

Jarovce (Croatian Hrvatski Jandrof, Hungarian Horvátjárfalu, German Kroatisch Jahrndorf) is a borough of okres Bratislava V between [[5-rusovce|Rusovce]] and [[5-petrzalka|Petržalka]], right by the Austrian border: the Jarovce–Kittsee crossing lies not far from the village. All four names say the same thing — the village is Croatian, and that is present tense, not past: in the 2011 census, of 1,438 inhabitants 231 gave Croatian as their mother tongue, and 179 spoke it at home.

The first written mention was contained in a donation charter of King Andrew II of 1208, confirming the foundation of the Benedictine abbey at Lébény. The charter itself has not survived, and is known from later ones — the oldest being the donation charter of King Louis I the Great of 1359, issued at Pécs: it confirms the privileges of the original document and adds grants of lands and settlements it did not contain.

Jarovce came to Slovakia late and for a particular reason. The village was joined to Czechoslovakia in 1947 by agreement with Hungary — together with Rusovce and [[5-cunovo|Čunovo]] — and the reason was the building of the Bratislava Danube port. The Croatian population clearly predominated then, and a considerable part of it still keeps its nationality, the speech of its ancestors and its folk customs. In 1947–1950 the village was administratively subordinate to neighbouring Rusovce, and it has been a borough of Bratislava since 1 January 1972.
