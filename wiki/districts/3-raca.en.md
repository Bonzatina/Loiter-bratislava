# Rača (Bratislava III)

Rača is the city's north-eastern borough at the foot of the Little Carpathians (Bratislava III okres), an ancient winegrowing village whose slopes are still covered with vineyards: Rača's Frankovka modrá is one of the great names of Little Carpathian wine. The old core with its winegrowers' houses lives alongside panel estates; a world of its own is the Rendez railway colony (Východné) on the eastern edge, by the Bratislava-East marshalling yard, where the [[Zeleznicne-muzeum-SR|Railway Museum of Slovakia]] works in the historic locomotive depot.

## Coat of Arms

![Coat of arms of Rača](/assets/erb-3-raca.png)

In a red field, two golden rampant lions hold a golden cross above a golden orb. The composition goes back to the old village seal of winegrowing Rača.
