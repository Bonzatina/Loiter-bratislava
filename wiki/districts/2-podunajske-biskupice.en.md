# Podunajské Biskupice (Bratislava II)

Podunajské Biskupice is Bratislava's south-eastern edge on the Danube plain (Bratislava II okres); until 1972 a separate village with centuries of history — the name betrays its medieval ownership by the archbishops of Esztergom (a "bishops' village"). Behind the panel housing, the old village core with its church and baroque manor houses survives — in one of them, the Juraj Albert curia on Linzbothova street, sits the [[Muzeum-obchodu|Museum of Trade]].

## Coat of Arms

![Coat of arms of Podunajské Biskupice](/assets/erb-2-podunajske-biskupice.png)

In a red field, on a green base, the figure of an archbishop in silver vestments and mitre, with a golden crozier and book. The arms literally retell the name of the "bishops' village" that belonged for centuries to the archbishops of Esztergom.
