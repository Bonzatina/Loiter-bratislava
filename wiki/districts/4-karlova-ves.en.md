# Karlova Ves (Bratislava IV)

Karlova Ves is a borough of okres Bratislava IV on the left bank of the Danube, between the Old Town and Dúbravka, squeezed between the river and the wooded slopes of the Little Carpathians. A former riverside village with a winegrowing past, it grew in the 20th century into a large residential district; today it holds university campuses, the zoo and the botanical garden, and along Devínska cesta — Danube oxbows and the island of Sihoť.

It is with Karlova Ves that the history of Bratislava's water supply begins: since 1886 the city has drunk water from the wells of Sihoť, pumped by the main pumping station on Devínska cesta — today the [[Vodarenske-muzeum|Waterworks Museum]]. The island remains an active source of drinking water to this day, which is why access to it is closed.

## Coat of Arms

![Coat of arms of Karlova Ves](/assets/erb-4-karlova-ves.png)

In a blue field, a golden letter "C" entwined with a grape bunch and tendrils. The letter refers to the borough's name (Karlova Ves — "Karl's village"; C for the Latin Carolus), and the grapes to the riverside village's winegrowing past.
