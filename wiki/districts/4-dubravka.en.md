# Dúbravka (Bratislava IV)

Dúbravka is the city's north-western borough between Lamač and the slopes of Devínska Kobyla (Bratislava IV okres). Like neighbouring Devínska, it is a former village settled in the 16th century by Croats fleeing the Ottomans — one of the four "Croatian villages" of today's Bratislava. In the 1970s–80s a large panel estate rose above the old core, but village Dúbravka has not vanished: winegrowers' yards survive by the church of Sts Cosmas and Damian, and its memory is collected by the [[Dubravske-muzeum|Dúbravka Museum]] in the former parish priest's house.

## Coat of Arms

![Coat of arms of Dúbravka](/assets/erb-4-dubravka.png)

In a blue field above a green base, a stone well with a golden windlass and a red bucket; a silver hand points at it from the right, while golden oak branches on the left allude to the borough's name (dúbrava — oak grove). The composition goes back to the old village seal.
