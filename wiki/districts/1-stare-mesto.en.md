# Staré Mesto (Old Town, Bratislava I)

Staré Mesto is the historic core of Bratislava and the only borough of the Bratislava I okres, on the left bank of the Danube at the foot of the castle hill. Almost everything associated with old Pressburg/Pozsony is concentrated here: the coronation route of the Hungarian kings, medieval streets, baroque palaces — and, layered on top, the architecture of the First Czechoslovak Republic, when the city first became the capital of Slovakia.

The interwar layer of the Old Town is most visible around SNP Square (Námestie SNP): here stand [[Manderlak|Manderlák]] — Slovakia's first high-rise — the [[Obchodny-dom-Dunaj|Dunaj department store (Brouk a Babka)]] and the [[Druzstevne-domy|"three red houses" of the cooperatives]]. This is the main cluster of Slovak [[functionalism]], linked to two architects: city native [[Ludwig-Christian|Christian Ludwig]] and the classic [[Bellus-Emil|Emil Belluš]]. Higher up on Palisády is [[Lutherov-dom|Luther House]]; by the Danube, the [[Propeler|ferry wharfs of the Propeler]]; on Župné Square the socialist era left Vladimír Dedeček's [[Najvyssi-sud-SR|Supreme Court]].

## Coat of Arms

![Coat of arms of Staré Mesto](/assets/erb-1-stare-mesto.png)

In a red field, a silver fortress: a crenellated wall with a gate and three embattled towers, the middle one taller. This is the historic coat of arms of Pressburg/Bratislava, known from medieval seals — Staré Mesto bears it as the direct heir of the old royal town.
