# Statue of St Florian (Socha sv. Floriána)

The statue of St Florian (Socha sv. Floriána) on Pri vinohradoch street in old [[3-raca|Rača]] is the baroque patron of firefighters, erected, it is believed, after the great fire that engulfed Rača in 1732. The modest village monument of the 18th century unexpectedly became famous across Slovakia — first through a scandalous repainting, then through an exemplary community rescue.

![The statue after the amateur repainting of 2024 that brought Rača Slovakia-wide fame](/assets/socha-svateho-floriana.jpg)

Florian is portrayed as a Roman soldier: one hand holds a flagstaff, with the other he pours water onto a burning house; the body stands in contrapposto, spiralling around its axis, the head turned to heaven — reduced but recognisable baroque. The statue, about 170 cm tall, stands on a 130-centimetre pedestal with a pear-shaped shaft; the plaque with the date 1876 probably records one of its renovations. The monument is listed in the city-wide register of local heritage (category 1A).

In 2024 an amateur repainting turned the saint, in the locals' words, "into a fairy-tale figurine rather than a symbol of the borough" — earning its author the Brutus 2024 anti-award, and the statue Slovakia-wide fame and an internet storm. The outrage turned into action: since the statue has no official owner (which legally complicates any repair — the borough is pressing the state to take over the abandoned object and then entrust it to Rača), the borough announced a public collection — and **128 donors raised €11,223**, with another €2,000 added as a grant by the VÚB Foundation. Mayor Michal Drotován announced the statue's move to a depository, where professional restoration will begin.
