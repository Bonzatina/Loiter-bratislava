# Tramín Kindergarten on Kadnárova (Materská škola Tramín)

Unique buildings still appear today: the kindergarten on Kadnárova street in [[3-raca|Rača]], finished in 2024, is a large circle a little over 32 metres across, set in the grass among preserved old trees. The name Tramín comes from a grape variety — out of Rača's own vocabulary; for its shape locals also call it "the UFO".

![Tramín Kindergarten on Kadnárova (Materská škola Tramín)](/assets/materska-skola-tramin.jpg)

The single-storey house is gathered around a generous inner atrium — "the heart of the kindergarten"; the atrium facade is fully glazed, while the outer wall is dressed in perforated corrugated metal working as a light membrane. Timber here is not a finish: glulam columns and beams together with CLT floor panels carry the whole building, and a green roof helps settle it into its surroundings. A dark plinth optically lifts the structure off the terrain; external sunshades cut summer overheating. The outward windows vary in shape, size and height — down to little gabled house forms — and this is what gives the kindergarten its playful character. All four classrooms face both outwards and into the atrium, so daylight comes from both sides and the spaces are visually linked. The design by the studio **PANTOGRAPH** was drawn in 2019–2023 and built in 2023–2024 for the Rača borough; the built area is 649 m², capacity up to 88 children, cost about €2.1 million. The layout is modular by intent: classrooms can be joined into larger spaces or divided into smaller ones, and in future the house could be converted into a community centre, a day facility for the elderly, a library or a cultural venue — the kindergarten was designed in advance to outlive its purpose.
