# Railway Museum of Slovakia (Železničné múzeum SR)

The Railway Museum of the Slovak Republic (Železničné múzeum SR) lives in the historic locomotive depot of Bratislava-East station — in the Rendez railway colony on the edge of Rača. Founded in 1983 as the departmental collection of the ČSD Eastern Railway, it grew into the country's main railway collection: steam locomotives from imperial-royal times to the 1950s, diesels and electrics, carriages, draisines, semaphores and the whole infrastructure of a classic depot with a turntable. Once a year, in June, Rendez resounds across the country with its steam locomotive festival, drawing engines from several countries.
