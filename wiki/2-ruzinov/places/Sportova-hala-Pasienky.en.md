# Pasienky Sports Hall (Športová hala Pasienky)

The Pasienky sports hall (Športová hala Pasienky) at Trnavská cesta 29 was Slovakia's first large sports venue with progressive engineering (1962, architect Jozef Chovanec, structural engineer Jozef Poštulka, contractor Priemstav). Its roof is cable-hung: steel cables tensioned in two perpendicular directions form a hyperbolic paraboloid surface suspended between two parabolic reinforced-concrete arches spanning 73.5 × 67.5 metres. The principle goes back to Maciej Nowicki's Raleigh arena (1948) — Pasienky became its Slovak embodiment.

![Pasienky hall — the parabolic arch and cable roof](/assets/hala-pasienky.jpg)

The elegant saddle-shaped silhouette made the hall an icon of the sports quarter: basketball and handball matches, concerts and fairs have filled it for decades. The hall still operates (its current commercial name is Gopass Aréna).
