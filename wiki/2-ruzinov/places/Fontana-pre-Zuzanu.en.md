# Fountain for Zuzana — the Fountain of Love (Fontána pre Zuzanu)

In the courtyard of the panel block bounded by Záhradnícka, Dohnányho, Šumavská and Kupeckého streets hides the city's most cinematic fountain — the "Fountain for Zuzana" (Fontána pre Zuzanu), a.k.a. the Fountain of Love. Architect Miloš Kalina built it in 1984–1985 as a set for the cult musical film of the same name about first love: a concrete chalice on a stem with a metal rose on a column, a circular basin 6.25 metres across with a low grooved wall. The "prop" had no plumbing — during filming, water was poured from a cistern so the spray would fly prettily over a couple in love.

![The "Fountain for Zuzana" in a Starý Ružinov courtyard](/assets/fontana-pre-zuzanu.jpg)

It stood dry for twenty-two years, until in 2007–2008, on the residents' initiative (and that of fountain enthusiast Peter Havaši), it was connected to the utilities and switched on for real — for the first time in its life. Today the fountain works, is well kept, and has been declared a local landmark of Ružinov — a small monument to how cinema becomes city.
