# Ceramic Sculpture with Three Figures (Keramická plastika s troma figúrami)

The Ceramic Sculpture with Three Figures (Keramická plastika s troma figúrami) by Mira Haberernová-Trančíková (1980) hides in the middle of a small park in the atrium of the secondary vocational school of technologies and crafts at Ivanská cesta 21. The totemic sculpture of glazed ceramics, 2.2 metres tall, is modelled as a surreal being with three heads jointly holding a sphere — a free, imaginative fusion of human and plant motifs: a flower, growth, the elements, forest fairies and other fairy-tale creatures.

![Ceramic Sculpture with Three Figures (Keramická plastika s troma figúrami)](/assets/keramicka-plastika-haberernovej.jpg)

The surface is cut through with leaf-shaped perforations and grooves like traces of the elements' flow; the monochrome palette is enlivened by coloured structures and runs of glaze. The work survives in good condition.
