# Jupiter of the Solar System Model (Jupiter)

The Jupiter of the [[solar-system-model|Solar System Model]] was installed on 26 February 2024 on Jelačičova street in front of the gymnasium building — 1.7 km from the [[Fontana-Planeta-mieru|fountain-"Sun"]] on Hodžovo Square. The system's largest planet is imposing even at 1:464 million: a golden sphere 30.8 cm across, set within a whole composition of stainless-steel railing frames. The walk here from the "Sun" covers almost the entire inner solar system: along the way you pass [[Model-Venuse|Venus]], the not-yet-realized Earth and [[Model-Marsu|Mars]], as well as the asteroid belt, which would fall roughly 900 metres out.

![Jupiter of the Solar System Model (Jupiter)](/assets/sustava-jupiter.jpg)

Jupiter holds 72% of the mass of all the system's bodies apart from the Sun, so with its installation the authors declared the model "99.92% complete". Light from the "Sun" takes 43 minutes to get here. The model was supported by the [[2-ruzinov|Ružinov]] borough and co-financed by the project's author Michal Páleník.
