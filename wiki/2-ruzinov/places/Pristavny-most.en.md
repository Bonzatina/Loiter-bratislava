# Prístavný Bridge (Prístavný most)

Prístavný Bridge (the Port Bridge, Prístavný most) is the two-storey workhorse among Bratislava's Danube bridges: below runs the railway with pedestrian and cycling paths, above a four-lane motorway. The project was in fact called the "motorway-railway bridge" (Diaľnično-železničný most), and from its opening until 1993 the bridge bore the name of the Heroes of Dukla (Most hrdinov Dukly) — in memory of the Second World War battle at the Dukla Pass; the current name comes from the neighbouring port.

![Prístavný Bridge (Prístavný most)](/assets/pristavny-most.jpg)

Construction began in 1977: by 1983 the lower, railway level was finished — allowing trains to finally leave the [[Stary-most|Old Bridge]] in 1985 — and in 1985 the upper, road level opened too. The four-span truss girder with a 204.8-metre main span weighs about 12 thousand tons over its 460-metre length. Designed for 50 thousand cars a day, today the bridge carries more than 120 thousand — the busiest junction of the city, part of the D1 motorway.
