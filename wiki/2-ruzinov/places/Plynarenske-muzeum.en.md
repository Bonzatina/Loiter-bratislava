# Slovak Gas Museum (Slovenské plynárenské múzeum)

The Slovak Gas Museum (Slovenské plynárenské múzeum) is the corporate museum of SPP (Slovenský plynárenský priemysel) on its campus at Mlynské nivy 44 — where Bratislava's gas industry has lived since the 19th century. It all began with the exhibition "In the Light of Gas Lamps" (1992); out of it grew a museum documenting a century and a half of the industry: from the town gasworks that lit Pressburg from 1856 to the transmission pipelines that made Slovakia a key European transit country. The collection holds gas lanterns and meters, gas stoves and refrigerators, models of works and compressor stations.
