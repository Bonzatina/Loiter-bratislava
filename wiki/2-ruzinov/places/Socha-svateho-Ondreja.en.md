# Statue of St Andrew (Socha sv. Ondreja)

The statue of St Andrew (Socha sv. Ondreja) in the park at the corner of Miletičova and Záhradnícka is a baroque migrant from the old town: for almost two centuries the saint stood on a corner pedestal of a large city building at the crossing of Štúrova and Gorkého streets, until in the late 1930s the house was slated for demolition to make way for the planned seat of the Slovak National Bank. The statue was taken down from the corner before the demolition, restored in 1942 at the city's expense and set on a new pedestal in the Ružinov park.

The inscription of that time on the pedestal dates the statue to 1712, while the literature gives 1742 — the truth, by different accounts, lies between them. The same inscription keeps the memory of the original address: in the 18th century the crossing where the saint stood was called St Andrew's street — today's Gorkého.
