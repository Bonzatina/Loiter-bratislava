# Andrej Hlinka Park (Park Andreja Hlinku)

Andrej Hlinka Park (Park Andreja Hlinku) is a linear park along Ružinovská street, running through the whole Trávniky housing district — a peak of Slovak landscape architecture. [[Milucky-Ferdinand|Ferdinand Milučký]] laid it out in 1978, a decade after the district itself, developing the devices found at Trávniky and the [[Krematorium|crematorium]]: plastic remodelling of the terrain articulated by clean lines of concrete walls, a play of the linear and the curved.

![The "veil" fountain in Andrej Hlinka Park](/assets/park-andreja-hlinku.jpg)

Two fountains hold the composition: the "veil" fountain (sculptor Jozef Vachálek) doubles as a music pavilion, while the "fan" fountain (Júlia Kunovská and Ladislav Mandíček) marks the park entrance. The park was originally dedicated to the communist Karol Šmidke — his statue no longer dominates the composition, and the park bears Andrej Hlinka's name. It remains Ružinov's main public space.
