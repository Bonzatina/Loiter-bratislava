# Salesian House on Miletičova (Saleziáni Don Bosca, Miletičova)

The Salesian house at Miletičova 7 (Saleziáni Don Bosca, Miletičova) is the third house of the Salesians of Don Bosco in Slovakia (the order has worked in the country since 1924) and the spiritual centre of a whole era: the order bought the land at Tehelné pole in 1930, construction began in October 1932, and on 14 September 1933 the Salesians moved in. On 8 December 1933 the city pastor Ľudovít Okánik blessed the chapel of Mary Help of Christians (Panna Mária Pomocnica kresťanov); the first director charged the community to "create an oasis" of joyful youth work "out of this place of silence and death" — the old brick pits still lay nearby.

![Salesian House on Miletičova (Saleziáni Don Bosca)](/assets/saleziani-mileticova.jpg)

The Salesians of Don Bosco (Saleziáni Don Bosca, SDB) are a male congregation of the Catholic Church, officially the "Society of St Francis de Sales" (hence the name "Salesians"). It was founded in Turin in 1859 by St John Bosco (Giovanni Bosco, 1815–1888), who devoted himself to the education of youth, above all in working-class industrial districts — exactly what Bratislava's Tehelné pole was. Today it is one of the church's largest congregations: some 13,500 members, including over a hundred bishops and a dozen cardinals. The Salesians came to Slovakia in 1924 (their first house was Šaštín) and managed to open thirteen houses before the communists took power; the Miletičova house, the third of them, became the principal one.

The house quickly became the all-Slovak centre of the Salesians: a chapel, halls, sports grounds, from 1934 the football club ŠK Konkordia and the editorial office of the Don Bosco magazine, and 500–700 youngsters a week in the oratory. From 1 September 1939 it housed the newly established Slovak Salesian inspectorate headed by Jozef Bokor. Everything ended on the "barbaric night": on 14 April 1950 armed police arrested the Salesians, the house was seized for offices, garages grew over the playing fields, and the chapel and oratory were destroyed. For forty years the order worked underground in Bratislava, out of private flats.

In 1990 the building was returned in a deplorable state; under director Štefan Wallner (from March 1991) a major reconstruction began: on 31 January 1993 a new public chapel opened, and on 19 October 1997 the new Church of Mary Help of Christians (Kostol Panny Márie Pomocnice kresťanov) was consecrated — the parish returned to its place. Today Miletičova once again hosts the provincial headquarters of the Slovak Salesians, a youth oratory, a publishing house and a pastoral centre — one of Bratislava's three Salesian houses (with Trnávka and Mamateyova).
