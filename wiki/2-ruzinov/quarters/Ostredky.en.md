# Ostredky

Ostredky is Ružinov's greenest residential quarter, a 1960s panel sídlisko that has managed not to be "densified" in sixty years. Aerial photographs of 1955 still show nothing but fields here; on 20 August 1964 the Večerník daily ran a front-page photo captioned "Construction begins at Ostredky", and by Christmas 1964 the first residents had moved into their new flats. In two years 3,400 flats went up — and over the following six decades only about 1,400 were added: today almost 8,400 people live on 0.986 km² (a fifth of them seniors).

![A mural on a panel block amid Ostredky's greenery](/assets/ostredky.jpg)

The quarter's signature feature is its "space" streets: Mesačná (Moon), Sputniková, followed by Družicová (Satellite), Polárna and Galaktická. A toponymic curiosity: Poludníková (Meridian) street runs almost parallel to the equator, while Rovníková (Equator) street runs parallel to the meridian.
