# Solar System Model (Potulky slnečnou sústavou)

"Strolls through the Solar System" (Potulky slnečnou sústavou) is a growing scale model of the solar system in Bratislava and its surroundings at 1:464,000,000. The scale is kept throughout — both in the sizes of the bodies and in the distances between them: the Sun comes out at 3 metres, the Earth at 2.75 cm, the Moon at 7.5 mm, and between the Sun and the Earth lie 322 metres of city streets. The project has been run since 2023 by Michal Páleník; individual planets were supported by grants from the Staré Mesto and Ružinov boroughs.

The project's Sun is the three-metre sphere in front of the [[Grasalkovicov-palac|Presidential Palace]] — the [[Fontana-Planeta-mieru|"Planet of Peace" fountain]] on [[Hodzovo-namestie|Hodžovo Square]]. Further across the city stand the project's own objects: [[Model-Venuse|Venus]] on May 1st Square (233 m from the Sun), [[Model-Marsu|Mars]] on [[Namestie-slobody|Freedom Square]] (491 m) and [[Model-Jupitera|Jupiter]] on Jelačičova street in [[2-ruzinov|Ružinov]] (1.7 km). The authors joke that since the Sun is 99.87% of the system's mass and Jupiter is 72% of the remainder, the model is "99.92% complete".

The continuation is planned outward: Saturn would fall 3.1 km from the Sun, Uranus 6.2 km, and for Neptune (9.7 km — the centre of Vajnory) the "Vajnory Neptune" project has been announced — a stainless-steel model of the planet with its major moons. The Kuiper belt at this scale is a ring 13–22 km from Hodžovo Square, and the aphelion of Halley's Comet falls on Ivanka pri Dunaji. There are also "bonus" mini-models indoors: Venus in the FunTastic shop, Mars in Foodstock and the Staré Mesto Library, Saturn in the iHRYsko shop — plus one "hidden" Mars the website invites you to find yourself.

The creators ask you to walk the model slowly: an ordinary walking pace at this scale is twice the speed of light. Guided walks along the Sun → Jupiter route take place occasionally.
