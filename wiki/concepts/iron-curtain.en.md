# The Iron Curtain

Bratislava was the only capital of the Soviet bloc standing directly on the Iron Curtain: the border with Austria ran along the Danube and the Morava, right by Petržalka's panel blocks and under the walls of Devín. The Czechoslovak section of the curtain stretched 920 kilometres (the Slovak part — 104 km); it was guarded by the Border Guard, which grew from six thousand men in 1950 to 25 thousand by the mid-1960s. Over forty years, almost 49 thousand people were detained trying to cross the Czechoslovak-Austrian and Czechoslovak-West German border, and at least 288 died, of them at least 42 on the Slovak section; near Petržalka, Kopčany and Jarovce alone — at least 19, from the Ehrenfeld family, shot in December 1952, to eighteen-year-old Hartmut Tautz, run down by dogs in August 1986. Their memory is kept by the [[Park-obeti-Hraniciarov|Park of the Border's Victims]] with barbed wire from the real curtain.

There were successful escapes too. The Pole Marcin Bieniada, a 23-year-old hater of the regime, spent the summer of 1982 scouting the border at Devín "as a tourist", watching from a tavern (today's U Srnčíka) and practising jumps from a lamppost in Šaľa with his sister. On the evening of 23 August 1982 he took bus no. 29 from the [[Most-SNP|SNP Bridge]] to Devín, waited for darkness, leapt from a lamppost over the signal fence without touching the alarm bar, crawled under the coils of barbed wire, waded the floodlit river branch neck-deep, swam the Morava — and climbed out on the Austrian bank. He returns every year to the site of "the most important day of his life".

After 1989 the barriers were torn down, and the former dead zone became a place of freedom: cycle paths along the Morava and the Danube, a Cold War museum set up by volunteers in [[Petrzalske-bunkre|bunker B-S 2]] (lost to the 2013 flood), memorials to the victims. The curtain is the dark reverse of socialist Petržalka's "peace" rhetoric: while the [[Holubica-mieru|Dove of Peace]] greeted guests from the south, a hundred metres away they were shooting their own people.
