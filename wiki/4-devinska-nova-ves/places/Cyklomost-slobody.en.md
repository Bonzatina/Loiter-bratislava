# Freedom Cycling Bridge (Cyklomost slobody)

The Freedom Cycling Bridge (Cyklomost slobody) is a cycling-and-pedestrian bridge over the Morava that in September 2012 connected Devínska Nová Ves with Austrian Schlosshof. Where only a quarter-century earlier the forbidden strip of the [[iron-curtain|Iron Curtain]] had run, a light steel ribbon now stretches 525 metres long and a mere 4 metres wide (weighing 593 tonnes); the design is by Milan Beláček, and construction was paid mostly from EU funds. Couples fasten love locks to the railings, [[Hrad-Devin|Devín Castle]] is visible from the deck, and a walk to the Austrian bank and back takes about an hour.

![Freedom Cycling Bridge (Cyklomost slobody)](/assets/cyklomost-slobody.jpg)

The bridge's name is a legend of its own. In February 2012 the Bratislava Region held a public vote, and "Chuck Norris Bridge" (Most Chucka Norrisa) won by a wide margin. The name also needed Austrian approval — and instead of the Texas ranger the Austrians proposed a "Maria Theresa Bridge", recalling that a bridge had already stood here in the empress's times. In the end neither was adopted: after the interim "Cycling Bridge Devínska Nová Ves – Schlosshof", the regional deputies settled on "Freedom Cycling Bridge"; in German it is the Fahrradbrücke der Freiheit. The bridge has closed at least twice: in summer 2013 the flooding Morava cut it off from Na mýte street, and in 2020 a truck driver ignored the signs and drove across the pedestrian bridge.

The bridge is an easy walk from Devínska Nová Ves (MHD stop Uhrovecká); Na mýte street has snack kiosks, with playgrounds, a memorial to the victims of the Iron Curtain and a memorial to St John of Nepomuk nearby, and right before the bridge entrance the [[Srot-park|Šrot Park gallery]] begins on the right. On the Austrian side the path leads to Schloss Hof — a baroque residence rebuilt by Eugene of Savoy and bought in 1755 by Maria Theresa (a second floor was added in 1773–1775); it is open daily 10:00–18:00, with tours available in Slovak.
