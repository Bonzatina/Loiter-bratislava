# Šrot Park Gallery (Galéria Šrot park)

Šrot Park (from šrot — scrap metal) is an open-air gallery at the entrance to the [[Cyklomost-slobody|Freedom Cycling Bridge]] in Devínska Nová Ves: dozens of life-size animals and fairy-tale creatures welded from old scrap — nuts, bolts and valves. The gallery's keeper Branislav Pokorný keeps expanding the collection: a wolf chases a hare here, an eagle lands in its nest, a badger guards its burrow, a snake slithers by, and a stag with branching antlers, kingfishers and even an exotic toucan pose for visitors.

![A scrap-metal stag in Šrot Park (Galéria Šrot park)](/assets/srot-park.jpg)

The gallery is open daily: weekdays 14:30–18:30, weekends 10:00–18:30. Admission is a voluntary contribution into the big black petrol canister at the entrance. Dogs must be leashed and the exhibits must not be touched: the rusty menagerie against the Morava floodplain forests nicely rounds off a walk across the bridge to Austrian Schlosshof.
