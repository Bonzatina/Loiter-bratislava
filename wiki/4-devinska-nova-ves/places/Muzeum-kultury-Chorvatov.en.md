# Museum of Croatian Culture in Slovakia (Múzeum kultúry Chorvátov na Slovensku)

The Museum of Croatian Culture in Slovakia (Múzeum kultúry Chorvátov na Slovensku) is a "minority museum" of the Slovak National Museum standing exactly where it belongs: at Istrijská 68 in Devínska Nová Ves, the most Croatian village of Slovakia. The exhibition tells of the Croats' northward migration in the 16th century, fleeing the Ottoman wars, and of four and a half centuries of their life in the new homeland: costumes, crafts, song tradition, and the history of the four Croatian villages of today's Bratislava — Devínska, Lamač, Dúbravka and Jarovce.

![Museum of Croatian Culture in Slovakia](/assets/muzeum-chorvatov.jpg)

The museum is the heart of a living community: the annual Festival of Croatian Culture takes place nearby.
