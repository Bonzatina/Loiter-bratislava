# Museum of Education and Pedagogy (Múzeum školstva a pedagogiky)

The Museum of Education and Pedagogy (Múzeum školstva a pedagogiky) is the museum of the history of the Slovak school, fittingly housed in a former school building at Charkovská 1 in Devínska Nová Ves (it moved here from Petržalka). The building is the former Masaryk State Folk School (Masarykova štátna ľudová škola) of 1926; after a partial reconstruction the museum reopened its doors on 27 April 2024. The museum itself dates from 1970 and is run by Slovakia's ministry of education; its mission is to collect, preserve and present material relics of schooling from the whole territory of present-day Slovakia. The tradition of school museums in the country goes back to the teacher Ľudovít Rizner and exhibitions of the late 19th century; the idea of a national pedagogical museum was promoted in 1925 by the future education minister Jozef Sivák.

![The museum's new seat — a former school in Devínska Nová Ves](/assets/muzeum-skolstva.jpg)

The permanent exhibition "Through the History of Schooling" (Dejinami školstva) leads from the Middle Ages to the 1990s: a recreated historic classroom, charters and textbooks, a gallery of teachers, scholars and officials who shaped the Slovak school, and touching exhibits such as the "rooster" primer (abecedár "kohútik") — an aid for teaching the basics of reading and counting. Part of the collections is also shown in virtual tours on the museum's website.
