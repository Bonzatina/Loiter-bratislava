# Museum of Education and Pedagogy (Múzeum školstva a pedagogiky)

The Museum of Education and Pedagogy (Múzeum školstva a pedagogiky) is the museum of the history of the Slovak school, fittingly housed in a former school building at Charkovská 1 in Devínska Nová Ves (it moved here from Petržalka). The tradition of school museums in Slovakia goes back to the teacher Ľudovít Rizner and exhibitions of the late 19th century; the idea of a national pedagogical museum was promoted in 1925 by the future education minister Jozef Sivák. The collection is desks and slates, primers and globes, school uniforms and teaching aids: the classroom as it was under the monarchy, the First Republic and socialism.

Address: Charkovská 1. Hours: every Thursday 13:00–19:00. **Admission free.** Wheelchair accessible. Details at msap.sk.
