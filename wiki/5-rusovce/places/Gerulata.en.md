# Gerulata — Roman Military Camp

Gerulata was a Roman military camp (castellum) on the Danube Limes, the empire's northern frontier, active from the 2nd to the 4th century. It belonged to the defensive system of the province of Pannonia and guarded a Danube crossing; a civilian settlement and cemeteries grew up beside the camp. This is Bratislava's principal Roman site: since 2021 the ruins have been part of the UNESCO World Heritage property "Frontiers of the Roman Empire — Danube Limes".

![Ruins of Gerulata](/assets/gerulata.jpg)

The surviving core — the foundations of a late-Roman 4th-century fort built into the corner of the earlier camp — is conserved in the open air amid the village streets of Rusovce, a couple of hundred metres from the [[Rusovsky-kastiel|mansion]]. The finds — gravestones, altars, weapons, ceramics and jewellery — are shown at the Ancient Gerulata museum (Antická Gerulata, Gerulatská 7), a site of the [[Muzeum-mesta-Bratislavy|Bratislava City Museum]]; open in season (usually May to October), wheelchair accessible.
