# Sysľovské Fields (Sysľovské polia)

Sysľovské polia (from syseľ — ground squirrel) is a protected bird area (CHVÚ, Natura 2000) on Bratislava's southernmost edge, at the junction of the boroughs of Jarovce, Rusovce and [[5-cunovo|Čunovo]], by the triple border of Slovakia, Austria and Hungary. This open steppe-like plain amid farmland is Slovakia's only nesting ground of the great bustard (drop veľký), Europe's heaviest flying bird. The red-footed falcon (sokol kobcovitý) has returned here too: once a common species, by 2012 it no longer bred here at all, and thanks to a long-term rescue programme the territory again holds 15–25 pairs.

![Sysľovské Fields (Sysľovské polia)](/assets/syslovske-polia.jpg)

The interior can only be entered along a seasonal educational trail the city opened on 1 July 2023: it operates annually from 1 July to 30 September for pedestrians and cyclists, movement is allowed strictly along the route, and outside the season entry to the protected area is forbidden — the birds are left in peace for the nesting time. The information panels along the way tell more than bird stories: the first Roman settlers lived here (the [[Gerulata|Gerulata]] camp is next door), the Laura farmstead (Laura majer) stood here, and for decades the life of the border villages was maimed by the [[iron-curtain|Iron Curtain]].
