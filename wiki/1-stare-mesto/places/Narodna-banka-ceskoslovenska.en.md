# Czechoslovak National Bank (Národná banka československá)

The branch building of the Czechoslovak National Bank (Národná banka československá) at Štúrova 2 was built in 1938 to a 1936 design by [[Bellus-Emil|Emil Belluš]]. It is the most "state-like" of his works: a monumental volume clad entirely in travertine — roughly finished at the base and smooth on the upper floors — was meant to radiate the reliability and authority of the young state. A statue of Hermes was intended for the void of the large entrance frontage; the stainless steel gate with square panelling survives.

![Czechoslovak National Bank (Národná banka československá)](/assets/narodna-banka-ceskoslovenska.jpg)

Stylistically the building reflects the late-1930s turn from pure [[functionalism]] towards monumentality — the influence of Italian Novecento and the German architecture of those years is evident. In 1939–1945 it housed the Slovak National Bank of the first Slovak Republic. Today the building is occupied by the Prosecutor General's Office of Slovakia.
