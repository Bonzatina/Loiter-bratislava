# Museum of Hungarian Culture in Slovakia (Múzeum kultúry Maďarov na Slovensku)

The Museum of Hungarian Culture in Slovakia (Múzeum kultúry Maďarov na Slovensku) is the museum of the country's largest national minority, housed in the Renaissance Brämer curia (Brämerova kúria) at Žižkova 18. The permanent exhibition "Traditions and Values" leads through a millennium of Hungarian presence on Slovak territory — from the conquest of the homeland to the 20th century: noble and peasant culture, book heritage, and the memory of coronation-era Pozsony.

Address: Žižkova 18 (Brämerova kúria). Hours: Tue–Sun 10:00–17:00 (last entry 16:30). Ticket: €4 / reduced €2; the joint SNM Žižkova ticket applies.
