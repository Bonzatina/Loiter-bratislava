# Statue of Imrich Karvaš (Socha Imricha Karvaša)

The statue of Imrich Karvaš (Socha Imricha Karvaša) in front of the headquarters of the National Bank of Slovakia is a five-metre granite monument weighing 13 tons by the sculptor Štefan Papčo, erected by the bank to its first governor. "In raw-broken stone you see character in its purest form — and in that I felt a parallel with Karvaš's personality: steadfastness, principle, a considered goal," the author explains the concept.

![The National Bank of Slovakia tower, before which the monument stands](/assets/socha-imricha-karvasa.jpg)

Imrich Karvaš was an outstanding economist, professor and banker who in one lifetime stood against two totalitarian regimes: during the war he financed the Slovak National Uprising, for which the Nazis arrested him and sentenced him to death, and after the war the communist regime threw him into prison once more. At the ceremonial unveiling, attended by the state's highest officials, NBS governor Peter Kažimír said: "Today we symbolically return to him the place in our history that is rightfully his."

The monument completes the city's "state" corner: the statue stands on the street bearing Karvaš's name, a block from the [[Slovensky-rozhlas|Slovak Radio pyramid]] and [[Namestie-slobody|Freedom Square]].
