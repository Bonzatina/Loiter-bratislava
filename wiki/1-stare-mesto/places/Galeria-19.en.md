# Gallery 19 (Galéria 19)

Gallery 19 (Galéria 19) is a non-commercial contemporary art gallery on Lazaretská street 19, which gave it its name. It was opened in October 2010 by the artist Marián Mudroch (1945–2019), one of Slovakia's notable conceptualists, and his founding text still serves as its programme: the place should become a genius loci — a point of encounters and understanding where art "stimulates, cultivates and makes us better beings", for "sensitivity is the definition of life". Behind the manifesto stands a quite practical principle: an auteur's dramaturgy of exhibitions amid a diversity of artistic views.

![The exhibition hall of Gallery 19 (Galéria 19)](/assets/galeria-19.jpg)

Exhibitions in the white ground-floor halls change roughly once a month; the programme brings together different generations of the Slovak scene — from authors of the neo-avant-garde circle to young names. Legally the gallery lives as the non-profit GALÉRIA 19 n.o. and is funded among other things by 2% income-tax donations; it has published annual reports since 2009.

## Practical Information

Exhibition space: Lazaretská 19. Hours: Monday–Friday 14:00–19:00; **admission free**. In summer the gallery usually closes for holidays (in 2026 — from 6 July to 9 September). No wheelchair access; guided group visits by prior arrangement. Tel. +421 903 256 675, galeria@galeria19.sk. Surrounding parking is paid (PAAS zone, most spaces on nearby Jakubovo square).
