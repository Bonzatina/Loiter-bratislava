# The First Postbox (Prvá poštová schránka)

The First Postbox (Prvá poštová schránka) is a bronze sculpture by Ladislav Sabo (co-author Jozef Hobor) of 2001 in the upper part of Poštová street, right where it meets [[Obchodna-ulica|Obchodná]]. Two stylised girls' figures in relaxed poses — one perched with her feet up on top, the other seated below — lean on a vertical post carrying a functional letterbox inscribed "Slovenská pošta, schránka č. 1" with the Slovak coat of arms. Elongated proportions and soft, flowing modelling lend the figures elegance, though there is a certain descriptiveness to the treatment: the piece belongs to the same wave of genre bronze attractions that settled the city's streets around the turn of the 2000s ([[Cumil|Čumil]], the Walking Man, the Napoleonic soldier).

![The First Postbox on Poštová street (Prvá poštová schránka)](/assets/prva-postova-schranka.jpg)

The sculpture arrived with the comprehensive revitalisation of Poštová street in 2000, designed by the BKU studio (Michal Bogár, Ľubomír Králik, Ľudovít Urban) — the street was unified with red paving and furnished with world-class designer furniture: lamps by Jean-Michel Wilmotte, benches and bins by Philippe Starck, and along its whole length ground lights engraved with postal stamps of countries around the world were set into the pavement. Time has been harsher on the design than on the bronze: the lights by the postbox no longer work, some are smashed and filled with concrete, while the sculpture itself (signed "L. SABO 2001" on the foot of the post) is preserved but unmaintained. It lies within the Central City Area heritage zone; the object is managed by Marianum.
