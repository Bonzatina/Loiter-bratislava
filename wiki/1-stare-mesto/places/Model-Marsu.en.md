# Mars of the Solar System Model (Mars)

The Mars of the [[solar-system-model|Solar System Model]] stands on [[Namestie-slobody|Freedom Square]] (Námestie slobody) — 491 metres from the [[Fontana-Planeta-mieru|fountain-"Sun"]] in front of the Presidential Palace. Like [[Model-Venuse|Venus]], it is a metal pillar with a cone: the red planet at 1:464 million is a ball of just 14.6 mm, half the size of the "Earth". Both tiny Martian moons would sit right next to it: Phobos 2 cm from the planet, Deimos 5 cm. Light from the "Sun" takes 12 minutes 40 seconds to get here.

![Mars of the Solar System Model (Mars)](/assets/sustava-mars.jpg)

The model was supported by the Staré Mesto borough and co-financed by the Employment Institute. Mars has the largest "retinue" in the project: besides the pillar on the square there are copies in Foodstock and the Staré Mesto Library, and the project website invites you to track down one more "hidden" Mars yourself.
