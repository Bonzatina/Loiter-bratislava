# Ján Cikker Museum (Múzeum Jána Cikkera)

The Ján Cikker Museum (Múzeum Jána Cikkera) is the house-museum of one of the chief Slovak composers of the 20th century, author of the operas *Juro Jánošík* and *Resurrection*. It occupies the functionalist villa at Fialkové údolie 2 ("Violet Valley"), where Cikker lived and worked from 1947 until his death in 1989. The museum was created in 2004 by the Ján Cikker Foundation to preserve the composer's legacy in its authentic setting: the study with his piano, the library, scores and awards.

![The Cikker villa in Fialkové údolie](/assets/muzeum-jana-cikkera.jpg)

It is one of the city's most intimate museums — and a good excuse to see the quiet villa quarter under the castle hill, built up with interwar modernism.
