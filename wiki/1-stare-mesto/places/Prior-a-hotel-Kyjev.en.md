# Prior Department Store and Kyjev Hotel (Prior a hotel Kyjev)

The complex of the Prior department store with the Kyjev Hotel (Prior a hotel Kyjev) on Kamenné Square (Kamenné námestie 1 / Rajská 2) is the largest post-war intervention in Bratislava's centre and the city's chief manifesto of [[postwar-modernism|late modernism]]. [[Matusik-Ivan|Ivan Matušík]] won the 1960 competition and led the project in three stages: the three-sided, spread-out mass of the department store (1964–1968) is linked by a shared horizontal base to the vertical slab of the hotel (1973). Artists took part in the finishes — clocks by Jaroslav Kočiš, works by Vincent Hložník.

![The Kamenné Square complex: the base and the Kyjev Hotel tower](/assets/prior-kyjev.jpg)

The register of architecture describes the complex as a "generously modern spirit" with "simple dignity", balancing the bustle of the square. The department store still works (after Prior it housed Tesco and MY), the hotel is a recognisable silhouette on the skyline; developers and heritage defenders have argued for years over the future of the complex and all of Kamenné Square.
