# Swan Fountain (Labutia fontána)

The Swan Fountain (Labutia fontána) is the centrepiece and chief landmark of the [[Medicka-zahrada|Medical Garden]] (Medická záhrada) in Staré Mesto. In a round stone basin some fifteen metres across, shaped like a quatrefoil — or, as Bratislavans put it, a leaf and a flower — four stylised bronze swans with mighty half-open wings and slender necks stand on a two-tiered pedestal, arranged in opposing pairs; jets of varying patterns play between the figures. The fountain was built in 1986 to a design by the academic sculptor and architect Pavel Mikšík, a master who devoted his career to fountains, from drinking fountains to monumental ones.

![Swan Fountain (Labutia fontána)](/assets/labutia-fontana.jpg)

The swans arrived at the height of the garden's comprehensive 1985–1988 reconstruction, replacing on the same spot the Mária fountain of 1976 — a work of the same Mikšík. Together with the entrance sculptures and drinking fountains of the ensemble by [[Miklanek-Rastislav|Rastislav Miklánek]] and Mikšík, the fountain became part of the garden's new sculptural identity and is today protected within the city-centre heritage zone. A 2021 restoration added four bird-drinking basins along the rim of the pool.

In 2026 the fountain underwent another major overhaul as part of the "green-blue renewal" of the Medical Garden: professional artistic restoration was paired with a modernisation of the entire technology, machine room included, and Mikšík's two drinking fountains in the park were renovated alongside it. The Staré Mesto borough scheduled the ceremonial relaunch, with a summer celebration by the fountain, for the evening of 28 July 2026.
