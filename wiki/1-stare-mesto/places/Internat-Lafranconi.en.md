# Lafranconi Dormitory (Internát Lafranconi)

The Lafranconi student dormitory and canteen (Internát Lafranconi) at Nábrežie arm. gen. L. Svobodu 9 was **Slovakia's first university dormitory** and one of the country's first realisations of the modern movement (designed by [[Silinger-Klement|Klement Šilinger]] in the late 1920s, completed in 1931). The five-storey residential wing opens its rooms south towards the Danube; the cylinder of a unique oval staircase stands out in the composition; the facades combine smallish windows, classical stucco and ceramic tile cladding — modernism still in kid gloves.

![The Lafranconi dormitory shortly after completion, archival photo](/assets/internat-lafranconi.jpg)

In the late 1950s a swimming pool was added to the complex — designed by the young [[Matusik-Ivan|Ivan Matušík]]. The building still serves students: it houses the dormitory of Comenius University's Faculty of Physical Education and Sports, and the name Lafranconi — after [[Lanfranconi-Enea|engineer Enea Lanfranconi]], tamer of the Danube, whose surname lost an "n" along the way — is also borne by the neighbouring Danube bridge.
