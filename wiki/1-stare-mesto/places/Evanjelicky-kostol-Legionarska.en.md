# Protestant Church on Legionárska (Evanjelický kostol)

The Protestant church at Legionárska 10 (Evanjelický kostol) in the Blumentál quarter was built in 1932 to [[Harminc-Milan-Michal|Milan Michal Harminc]]'s competition design of 1929 — his most unexpected church: instead of his usual historicism, a Constructivist grouping of prismatic masses with a rectangular tower beside the entrance. The sacred volume is clad in brick-red ceramic tiles, the base in rough-cut travertine.

![Protestant Church on Legionárska (Evanjelický kostol)](/assets/evanjelicky-kostol-legionarska.jpg)

Inside is a raised hall-space with side galleries and an organ loft at the rear. The church stands at the centre of an entire Evangelical complex: grouped with it around a courtyard are a parish house and a residence for Protestant students. The church still serves its original purpose.
