# Apartment Block for Postal Employees (Dom poštových zamestnancov)

The apartment block for postal employees (Dom poštových zamestnancov) at Legionárska 1 is a whole quarter of 1920s social housing by [[Silinger-Klement|Klement Šilinger]]: four wings fill the entire plot between Legionárska and Šancová, three facing the streets and the fourth an inner courtyard, from which four staircases distribute residents to their flats (one- to three-room, each with kitchenette, bath, WC and vestibule — a dignified standard for postmen by the era's measure).

![The postal employees' block, archival photo](/assets/dom-postovych-zamestnancov.jpg)

The facades carry the Cubist-Constructivist decor of [[rondocubism|Rondocubism]]: flat framing, a stepped portal above the through-passage, circular "targets" on the corner loggias and Cubist ornament on the balcony railings. The house lives on, still residential.
