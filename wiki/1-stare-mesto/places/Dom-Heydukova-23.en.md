# Apartment Block at Heydukova 23 (Nájomný dom, Heydukova 23)

The five-storey apartment house at Heydukova 23 (1923) is an intimate example of [[Silinger-Klement|Klement Šilinger]]'s [[rondocubism|Rondocubism]]. L-shaped in plan, entered through an underpass, it holds a symmetrical street facade: three vertical strips rise into three half-arched gables above the cornice, balconies project on the first three floors, and the ground-level reliefs echo the composition in circular motifs. The courtyard facade is subtler — understated three-dimensional framing; the circle and semicircle recur in the interior details too.

![The house at Heydukova 23](/assets/dom-heydukova-23.jpg)

The house stands a few plots from the synagogue and is still residential.
