# Planet of Peace Fountain (Fontána Planéta mieru)

The Planet of Peace Fountain (Fontána Planéta mieru, also simply Fontána mieru — the Peace Fountain) is a three-metre steel sphere in a round basin on [[Hodzovo-namestie|Hodža Square]], in front of the railings of the [[Grasalkovicov-palac|Grassalkovich Palace]], the residence of the President of Slovakia. Its author is the sculptor Tibor Bártfay; relief doves fly across the polished sphere and human hands reach out to one another. The fountain's name is a memento of the era when the square itself was called Peace Square (Mierové námestie): the "planet of peace" was its symbolic centre, and for many Bratislavans the fountain is still the defining landmark of "Mierko".

![The Planet of Peace fountain in front of the Presidential Palace (Fontána Planéta mieru)](/assets/sustava-slnko.jpg)

In the 2020s the sphere gained a second, astronomical life: the [[solar-system-model|"Strolls through the Solar System"]] project adopted it as the Sun of its 1:464 million scale model — the real Sun with its 1,392,000 km diameter shrinks in it to almost exactly 300 cm. The model's planets are now measured from the fountain: Mercury would fall 125 metres away, [[Model-Venuse|Venus]] stands 233 metres away on May 1st Square, the Earth would be a 2.75 cm ball at 322 metres, [[Model-Marsu|Mars]] is 491 metres away on Freedom Square, and [[Model-Jupitera|Jupiter]] is 1.7 km away, already in Ružinov. The scale makes the proportions tangible: light from this "Sun" would take 8 minutes 19 seconds to reach the "Earth", the Parker Solar Probe in December 2024 approached the real Sun to a distance equal to 16 metres here, and the sphere's mass, were it to scale by weight as well, would be almost 20 tonnes — 99.87% of the mass of the whole system.
