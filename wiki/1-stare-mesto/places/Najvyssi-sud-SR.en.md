# Supreme Court of the Slovak Republic (Najvyšší súd SR)

The building of the Supreme Court of the Slovak Republic (Najvyšší súd SR) at Župné námestie 13 is a late work by [[Dedecek-Vladimir|Vladimír Dedeček]]: designed in 1977–1978, its construction (contractor Stavoindustria) stretched from 1984 to 1991, so the building opened in a different country. It is a six-storey administrative complex with a three-tract layout around an internal atrium: on the ground floor, under natural skylighting, eight courtrooms — two large halls seating 150 and smaller ones for about 50; above, the administration of the court and the justice ministry.

![Supreme Court of the Slovak Republic (Najvyšší súd SR)](/assets/najvyssi-sud.jpg)

The structure is a prefabricated reinforced-concrete skeleton with brick infill; the facades blend light Croatian sandstone with precast concrete panels. The north and south facades cantilever outwards, forming covered walkways; the western one is drawn with horizontal balcony lines and a marquee over the main entrance. The artistic programme is typical of the era: the relief "Will of the People" (Vôľa ľudu) by Alexander Trizuliak on the main facade, stained glass by Ferdinand Hložník in the corridors, wooden interiors by Jaroslav Nemec. The building still serves the court.
