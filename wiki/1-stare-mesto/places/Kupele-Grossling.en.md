# The Grössling Baths (Kúpele Grössling)

The Grössling Baths (Kúpele Grössling) on Kúpeľná street are the legendary city baths of 1895, one of the last unrestored national cultural monuments in the centre of Bratislava. Generations of townspeople came here to bathe, swim and socialize until the baths closed in 1994; since then the building has waited decades for a second life.

![The Grössling baths building on Kúpeľná street](/assets/kupele-grossling.jpg)

The city took up the revival: in 2019 the Metropolitan Institute of Bratislava launched an international architectural competition, won by the Florentine studio OPPS Architettura. The project returns the building to its bathing function — with a swimming section, baths and a library — but the road is hard: deadlines slipped over design delays and neighbours' objections, building materials rose in price by about 35% in three years, and the expected investment grew to ~€20 million. On the schedule as of late 2023, completion of the works was expected at the end of 2026, with the renewed baths opening in the first half of 2027; the demolition of later courtyard additions is already done — more than 1,500 tonnes of accretions "stuck" to the building over half a century were carried out by hand — the historic chimney of 1914 has been restored, the rare steam boilers of 1912 conserved, and the clearance revealed precious details such as the attic windows and the anchoring of the corner skylight. The entrance will return to the corner of Kúpeľná and Medená streets — as in 1895; alongside the baths the building will also house a library.
