# Museum of Carpathian German Culture (Múzeum kultúry karpatských Nemcov)

The Museum of Carpathian German Culture (Múzeum kultúry karpatských Nemcov) is one of the Slovak National Museum's "minority museums" in the row of curias on Žižkova street (no. 14) under the castle hill. The exhibition tells the eight-century story of the Germans of Slovakia: from the medieval colonisation of the mining towns and Pressburg through their craft and winegrowing heyday to the tragic finale — the deportations of 1945–1946. The museum also has branches in the historic centres of German settlement — the meeting houses in Handlová and Nitrianske Pravno (the Hauerland region).

![Curias on Žižkova street](/assets/muzeum-karpatskych-nemcov.jpg)

For Bratislava this is partly a museum about itself: for centuries Germans were Pressburg's largest community, and their trace — from wineries to architecture — runs through this whole wiki.

## Practical Information

Address: Žižkova 14. SNM Žižkova exhibition regime: Tue–Sun 10:00–17:00; the joint ticket for the Žižkova street museums applies.
