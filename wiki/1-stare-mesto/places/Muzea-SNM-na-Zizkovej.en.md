# SNM Museums on Žižkova (Múzeá SNM na Žižkovej)

The SNM Museums on Žižkova (Múzeá SNM na Žižkovej) are a museum complex of the Slovak National Museum in a conjoined row of Renaissance curias under the castle hill (formally nos. 12–18 Žižkova street): a surviving fragment of the demolished [[Zuckermandel]], where four museums live under one roof with a joint ticket. Here the SNM gathered its "minority museums" — a portrait of multinational Pressburg — and added archaeology and music to them.

![Curias on Žižkova street](/assets/muzeum-karpatskych-nemcov.jpg)

## The Four Museums

**The Archaeological Museum (Archeologické múzeum SNM)** in the Kamper curia (Kamperova kúria, no. 12) runs from the Palaeolithic to the Slovak Middle Ages: stone tools, Bronze Age hoards, Roman and Germanic antiquities, and a lapidarium of tombstones and altars.

**The Museum of Carpathian German Culture (Múzeum kultúry karpatských Nemcov)** (no. 14) tells the eight-century story of the Germans of Slovakia: from the medieval colonisation of the mining towns and Pressburg through their craft and winegrowing heyday to the tragic finale — the deportations of 1945–1946. For Bratislava this is partly a museum about itself: for centuries Germans were the city's largest community, and their trace — from wineries to architecture — runs through this whole wiki. The museum also has branches in Handlová and Nitrianske Pravno (the Hauerland region).

**The Museum of Hungarian Culture in Slovakia (Múzeum kultúry Maďarov na Slovensku)** in the Brämer curia (Brämerova kúria, no. 18) is the museum of the country's largest national minority. The permanent exhibition "Traditions and Values" leads through a millennium of Hungarian presence on Slovak territory — from the conquest of the homeland to the 20th century: noble and peasant culture, book heritage, and the memory of coronation-era Pozsony.

**The Music Museum (Hudobné múzeum SNM)**, the Hungarians' neighbour in the Brämer curia, is one of the few specialised collections in Europe devoted to the history of music: an archive of scores, documents and instruments from folk to salon. For a city where Haydn, the child Mozart and Liszt performed, and where Hummel and Schmidt were born, it is a natural part of the biography. Its second site is the manor at Dolná Krupá, linked to the Brunsvik family; tradition has it that Beethoven stayed there. **Note: the exhibition may be closed for technical reasons** — check snm.sk before visiting.

## Practical Information

Address: Žižkova 12–18. Hours: Tue–Sun 10:00–17:00 (last entry 16:30). The joint ticket covers all SNM museums on Žižkova (Archaeological, Hungarian culture, Carpathian Germans, Music, Podhradie pavilion); individually: Archaeological €3 / €1.50, Hungarian culture €4 / €2. Children under 6 free.
