# Dunaj Department Store (Obchodný dom Dunaj)

The Dunaj department store (Obchodný dom Dunaj) at Námestie SNP 30 was built in 1936 for the Czech retail chain Brouk a Babka to a 1935 design by architect [[Ludwig-Christian|Christian Ludwig]]. The building is considered **one of the greatest works of Slovak [[functionalism]]**: a ferroconcrete skeleton with a free floor plan yielded seven above-ground and two underground floors of open retail halls — and the whole thing went up in five months, from April to August.

![Dunaj Department Store (Obchodný dom Dunaj)](/assets/obchodny-dom-dunaj.jpg)

A communications core with a dual staircase and paired elevators freed up the facade: ribbon windows framed in stainless steel run across its entire width, the walls are clad in ceramic tile, and the perimeter originally carried neon illumination. Three large vitrines on the ground floor form a striking entrance zone. The building consistently employs industrially prefabricated elements — cutting-edge construction technology for 1930s Slovakia.

After the war the store took the name "Dunaj" (Danube), under which the city knows it; in the late 1990s it was renovated to a design by architect Ján Bahna. The building survives and still serves trade and services.
