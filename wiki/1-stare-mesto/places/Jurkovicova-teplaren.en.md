# Jurkovič Heating Plant (Jurkovičova tepláreň)

The boiler room and turbine hall at Čulenova 7, known as the Jurkovič Heating Plant (Jurkovičova tepláreň), is Bratislava's chief monument of industrial modernism. The 1939 design was built from 1942 on the land of the Apollo mineral oil refinery and completed in stages into the 1970s: boiler house, turbine halls, waterworks, a transformer station, a control room and a chimney visible from afar. The cubic boiler house with lattice facades and corner towers suggests something between a castle and a giant transformer of the city.

![Jurkovič Heating Plant (Jurkovičova tepláreň) before renovation](/assets/jurkovicova-teplaren.jpg)

The name pins the authorship on [[Jurkovic-Dusan|Dušan Jurkovič]], but the attribution is in question: the 2008 architectural-historical research found no documentary confirmation of his authorship. The name stuck nonetheless — and saved the building: the plant was revitalised as the heart of the new Sky Park district (facade and roof restoration by the PAMARCH studio), with a separate five-storey glazed volume housing the BASE coworking space inserted inside.
