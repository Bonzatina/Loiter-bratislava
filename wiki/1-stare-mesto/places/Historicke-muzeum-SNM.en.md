# SNM Museum of History (Historické múzeum SNM)

The Museum of History of the Slovak National Museum (Historické múzeum SNM) is one of the largest and most important museums in the country, occupying the main halls of [[Bratislavsky-hrad|Bratislava Castle]] (Bratislavský hrad). It grew out of the SNM's historical department (1961), became the Historical Institute in 1970 and has borne its present name since 1989. Its collections span Slovak history from the Middle Ages to the 20th century: furniture, clocks, weapons, glass, ceramics, painting, folk and sacred art — the basis of the castle's permanent exhibitions and large temporary shows, while the castle terraces offer the canonical view of the Danube and the city.
