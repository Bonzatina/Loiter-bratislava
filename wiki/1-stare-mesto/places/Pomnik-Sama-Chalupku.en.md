# Samo Chalupka Monument (Pomník Sama Chalupku)

The Samo Chalupka Monument (Pomník Sama Chalupku) stands in a small park at the corner of Legionárska and Radlinského streets, on the north side of the Jur Hronec dormitory. The over-life-size bronze bust of the poet was set up in 1974 by sculptor Pavol Chrťan with co-author Julián Hauskrecht on a large round block of dressed stone 348 cm wide — a disc with a wedge cut out, resembling either a split tablet or a bitten sun. The bust is handled in a realistic-expressive manner, stressing the poet's sharp features; carved in the stone beneath is "Samo Chalupka 1812 – 1883".

![Samo Chalupka Monument (Pomník Sama Chalupku)](/assets/pomnik-sama-chalupku.jpg)

Samo Chalupka was a Slovak Romantic poet and evangelical priest, younger brother of the playwright Ján Chalupka, a member of the Štúr generation's Tatrín society, co-author of the Memorandum of the Slovak Nation and co-founder of Matica slovenská; his heroic poem "Mor ho!" became a schoolbook classic. The monument lies within the Central City Area heritage zone; it is well preserved, only slightly weathered, and the park around it is undergoing revitalisation.
