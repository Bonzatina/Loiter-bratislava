# Bratislava City Museum (Múzeum mesta Bratislavy)

The Bratislava City Museum (Múzeum mesta Bratislavy, MMB) is the oldest continuously operating museum in Slovakia: it was founded in 1868 on the initiative of the Bratislava City Beautification Society and mayor Justi. Its main seat is the [[Stara-radnica|Old Town Hall]] (Stará radnica, entrance from Radničná 1), which hosts the exhibition of city history: guild antiquities, coronation-era Pressburg, town law and everyday life; the town hall tower offers the best view of the Main Square.

![The Old Town Hall tower — the museum's main seat](/assets/muzeum-mesta-bratislavy.jpg)

The MMB is a whole constellation of sites across the city and beyond: the Apponyi Palace with its museums of viticulture and period interiors, the Red Crayfish pharmacy, the museum of arms in Michael's Tower and of clocks in the House of the Good Shepherd, Roman [[Gerulata]] in Rusovce and [[Hrad-Devin|Devín Castle]] above the confluence of the Morava and the Danube.

## Practical Information

Main exhibition address: Radničná 1. Hours vary by site — see muzeumbratislava.sk (tickets there too). Wheelchair access: Apponyi Palace, Gerulata, Devín.
