# Slovak National Museum (Slovenské národné múzeum)

The main building of the Slovak National Museum (Slovenské národné múzeum) on the Vajanský embankment (Vajanského nábrežie 2) was built in 1928 to a 1924 design by [[Harminc-Milan-Michal|Milan Michal Harminc]] — originally as the Agricultural Museum (Zemedelské múzeum). Harminc won a competition in which prominent Czechoslovak architects submitted modern, modernist proposals — but the jury chose his emphatically classical concept: a three-storey symmetrical volume with a pair of Doric columns carrying a large pediment.

![Slovak National Museum (Slovenské národné múzeum)](/assets/slovenske-narodne-muzeum.jpg)

The building's national programme was carried by sculpture: on the facade, the reliefs *Sowing* and *Harvest* (1926) by the Moravian sculptor Franta Úprka celebrated peasant labour. The building is regarded as Harminc's "purest" work and has lost none of its splendour; a monument to T. G. Masaryk stands before the entrance. Today it is the headquarters of the whole SNM network and home of its **Natural History Museum** (Prírodovedné múzeum) — millions of specimens of minerals, fossils, plants and animals, from the mammoths of Ice Age Slovakia to Danube fish.
