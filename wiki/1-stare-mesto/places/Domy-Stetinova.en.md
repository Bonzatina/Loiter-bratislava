# Apartment Blocks on Štetinová (Nájomné domy, Štetinova 1–5)

The apartment houses at Štetinova 1–3–5 (1922) in the Evangelical quarter are among [[Silinger-Klement|Klement Šilinger]]'s very best efforts and a concentrate of the [[rondocubism|Rondocubist]] vocabulary: dynamic portals, conical capitals, round "targets", Constructivist balconies — all in a rich colour scheme under a massive mansard roof.

![The portal of Štetinova 5](/assets/domy-stetinova.jpg)

The houses are still residential; for a Rondocubism hunter, Štetinová is a mandatory stop between the [[Anatomicky-ustav|Institute of Anatomy]] and the [[Dom-postovych-zamestnancov|postal employees' block]].
