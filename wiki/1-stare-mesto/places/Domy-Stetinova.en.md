# Apartment Blocks on Štetinová (Nájomné domy, Štetinova 1–5)

The apartment houses at Štetinova 1–3–5 (1922) in the Evangelical quarter are among [[Silinger-Klement|Klement Šilinger]]'s very best efforts and a concentrate of the [[rondocubism|Rondocubist]] vocabulary. The composition itself is built on traditional historical principles — and it is onto that base that the dynamic portals, pyramidal capitals, round "targets" and Constructivist balconies are applied. Šilinger heightened the effect of the façade thus set in motion with saturated colour, and the massive mansard roof is characteristic of this period of his work altogether.

![The portal of Štetinova 5](/assets/domy-stetinova.jpg)

The houses are still residential; for a Rondocubism hunter, Štetinová is a mandatory stop between the [[Anatomicky-ustav|Institute of Anatomy]] and the [[Dom-postovych-zamestnancov|postal employees' block]].

## Practical Information

- Address: Štetinova 1, 3, 5, Bratislava
- Access: residential buildings, to be seen from outside; the portals face straight onto the street
