# Memorial to the Victims of Communism (Pamätník obetiam komunizmu)

The Memorial to the Victims of Communism (Pamätník obetiam komunizmu) stands in the greenery at the western end of the small park on Jakubovo námestie. Like the [[Brana-slobody|Gate of Freedom]] at Devín, it was unveiled symbolically — on 17 November 2005, the Day of the Struggle for Freedom and Democracy. Its author is the noted Slovak sculptor Jozef Barinka, creator, among other things, of the trophy collection of the Slovak Ice Hockey Federation.

![Memorial to the Victims of Communism on Jakubovo námestie (Pamätník obetiam komunizmu)](/assets/pamatnik-obetiam-komunizmu.jpg)

The monument, 250 × 380 cm, is hewn from stone in the form of a broad crag on a low rectangular pedestal. Reliefs adorn the lower part: on the right, a doubled outline of a face and two palms cut through by bands — bars (the bands and the lower face are picked out in blue); on the left, the carved inscription OBETIAM KOMUNIZMU ("To the Victims of Communism"). The rear is formed by two stones set against each other, with a Latin cross carved on the larger one. The memorial is listed among the city's places of memory and lies within the protected zone of the central city area.
