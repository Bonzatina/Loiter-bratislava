# Coop Houses (Družstevné domy)

The Coop Houses (Družstevné domy), known as the "three red houses", are a complex of three connected buildings at Námestie SNP 13–15, built for farmers' cooperatives to a design by [[Bellus-Emil|Emil Belluš]]. They went up in stages in the second half of the 1930s: first the corner bank building (no. 15), almost simultaneously house no. 13, and the middle one last. In the 1950s the ensemble was completed by a fourth building by architect Miloš Chorvát.

![Coop Houses (Družstevné domy)](/assets/druzstevne-domy.jpg)

The upper floors are clad in distinctive red ceramics, the lower levels in stone; inside were a bank, cooperative headquarters, a cinema, shops, offices and flats. The street level is organised by two transverse passages and an arcade with an elliptical skylight — Belluš's typical balance of modernist structure and a dignified, "urban" facade. The complex is very much alive: functions have changed over the decades, but the mixed use — housing, offices, retail, entertainment — endures.
