# Ferdinand Milučký's House (Dom Ferdinanda Milučkého)

[[Milucky-Ferdinand|Ferdinand Milučký]]'s own house at Révová 54 (1975) is a compact manifesto of his philosophy: folk building as an "underlying sensibility" united with modern technique. In this house on the former winegrowing slopes of the Vinárky quarter, a modernist structure is dressed in vernacular materials and forms — the inserted windows, wooden and metal connecting details — while the space is built on transparency and a continuous relationship with nature: the garden flows into the interior and the patio without sacrificing the intimacy of the home.

![Milučký's house on Révová, archival photo](/assets/dom-miluckeho.jpg)

The house survives and remains lived-in — a quiet counterpart to the monumental [[Krematorium|crematorium]] by the same author, and a vivid answer to the question of how a modernist architect builds for himself.
