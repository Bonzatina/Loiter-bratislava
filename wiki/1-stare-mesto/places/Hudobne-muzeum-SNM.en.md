# SNM Music Museum (Hudobné múzeum SNM)

The Music Museum of the Slovak National Museum (Hudobné múzeum SNM) is one of the few specialised collections in Europe devoted to the history of music: an archive of scores, documents and musical instruments from folk to salon. The museum resides in the Renaissance Brämer curia (Brämerova kúria) at Žižkova 18, which it shares with the [[Muzeum-kultury-Madarov|Museum of Hungarian Culture]]. Its second site is the manor at Dolná Krupá, linked to the Brunsvik family; tradition has it that Beethoven stayed there.

![Brämer curia (Brämerova kúria)](/assets/hudobne-muzeum.jpg)

For a city where Haydn, the child Mozart and Liszt performed, and where Hummel and Schmidt were born, a music museum is a natural part of the biography.

## Practical Information

Address: Žižkova 18 (Brämerova kúria). Usual regime: Tue–Fri 10:00–17:00 (last entry 16:30), joint Žižkova museums ticket. **Note: the exhibition may be closed for technical reasons** — check snm.sk before visiting.
