# Tatra Bank Palace (Palác Tatra banky)

The Tatra Bank palace (Palác Tatra banky) at Námestie SNP 33 was built in the 1920s to a design by [[Harminc-Milan-Michal|Milan Michal Harminc]] — in a classicist spirit with baroque and art-nouveau details. The symmetrical facade is held by pairs of pilasters topped with neo-classical statues by sculptor Ján Koniarek. The bank occupied the first two floors: from the vestibule, visitors entered a grand two-storey banking hall under a skylight; above were apartments, at street level three leasable shops, and in the basement the bank vaults and a movie theatre with sublime art-nouveau ceramic decoration.

![Tatra Bank Palace (Palác Tatra banky), archival photo](/assets/palac-tatra-banky.jpg)

In 1936 architect Josef Konrad delicately fitted the Zinsenheim fashion outlet into the ground floor. The building still stands, having swapped bankers for ministry officials — it houses the Ministry of Culture of Slovakia; the mixed use (offices, housing, retail) endures.
