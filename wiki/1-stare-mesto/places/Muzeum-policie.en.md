# Police Museum of the Slovak Republic (Múzeum polície SR)

The Police Museum of the Slovak Republic (Múzeum polície SR) at Gunduličova 2 is the only specialised collection of its kind in the country, a departmental museum of the Ministry of the Interior. The exhibition documents the history of law enforcement from the gendarmerie of the First Republic to the modern police: uniforms, forensics (from fingerprinting to ballistics), famous investigations, the traffic service, and Iron Curtain border protection.

![Police Museum of the Slovak Republic on Gunduličova](/assets/muzeum-policie.jpg)

An amusing contrast: the museum of a stern agency hides in an ornate eclectic apartment house on a quiet street below the [[Grasalkovicov-palac|Presidential Palace]].

- Address: Gunduličova 2
- Hours: Tue–Fri 10:00–14:00 (last entry 13:30); closed Mon, Sat, Sun
- Tickets: admission free
- Groups: guided visits by appointment (muzeumpz@minv.sk)
