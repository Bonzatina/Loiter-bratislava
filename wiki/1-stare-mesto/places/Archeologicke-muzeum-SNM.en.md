# SNM Archaeological Museum (Archeologické múzeum SNM)

The Archaeological Museum of the Slovak National Museum (Archeologické múzeum SNM) occupies the Renaissance Kamper curia (Kamperova kúria) at Žižkova 12 — in the row of old sub-castle manors under the castle hill where the SNM has gathered several of its museums. The exhibitions run from the Palaeolithic to the Slovak Middle Ages: stone tools, Bronze Age hoards, Roman and Germanic antiquities, and a lapidarium of tombstones and altars.

![SNM Archaeological Museum (Kamperova kúria)](/assets/archeologicke-muzeum.jpg)

## Practical Information

Address: Žižkova 12. Hours: Tue–Sun 10:00–17:00 (last entry 16:30). Ticket: €3 / reduced €1.50; valid for all SNM museums on Žižkova street (Archaeological, Hungarian culture, Carpathian Germans, Music, Podhradie pavilion). Children under 6 free.
