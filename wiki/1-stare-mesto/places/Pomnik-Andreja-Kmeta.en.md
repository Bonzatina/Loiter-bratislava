# Andrej Kmeť Monument (Pomník Andreja Kmeťa)

The monument to Andrej Kmeť (Pomník Andreja Kmeťa) stands in the little park on Kmeťovo námestie in Blumentál — a white stone pillar out of which a face emerges. It was unveiled in 1971; the author is the academic sculptor Alina Ferdinandy.

![Andrej Kmeť Monument (Pomník Andreja Kmeťa)](/assets/pomnik-andreja-kmeta.jpg)

Kmeť (1841–1908) was a figure of almost impossible range: archaeologist, geologist, mineralogist, palaeontologist, historian, botanist, ethnographer and folklorist, he left a marked imprint on Slovak cultural, political and scholarly life in the second half of the 19th and the beginning of the 20th century, uniting in one person the spiritual, the scientific and the educational while remaining a bearer of the ideas of Matica slovenská. The inscription under the bust calls him the founder of Slovak museology and a pioneer of Slovak natural science, history and archaeology.

The monument is simply built: from a low platform of square plan rises a slender four-sided prism, in the upper part of which the naturalist's bust is carved. The material is white natural stone; the platform is concrete faced with stone slabs. After an outbreak of vandalism the monument was renewed — the conservation work was done by Andrej Baník, at a total budget of €1,300; the source gives no year for the work, saying only that it was finished at the end of May.
