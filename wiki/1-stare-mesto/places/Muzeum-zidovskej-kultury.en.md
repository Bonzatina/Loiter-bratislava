# SNM Museum of Jewish Culture (Múzeum židovskej kultúry SNM)

The Museum of Jewish Culture (Múzeum židovskej kultúry) of the Slovak National Museum occupies the Zsigray curia (Zsigrayova kúria) at Židovská 17 — one of the few surviving houses of the old Jewish quarter under the castle, demolished during the construction of the SNP Bridge. The exhibition leads through the religious and everyday life of Slovak Jews — synagogue silver, festivals, crafts — to the heaviest chapter: the Holocaust in Slovakia, the deportations of 1942 and the memory of 70,000 dead.

![The Zsigray curia on Židovská street](/assets/muzeum-zidovskej-kultury.jpg)

The museum's very location is part of the story: Židovská ("Jewish") street remains a narrow edge of a quarter erased in the 1960s; from its windows you see St Martin's Cathedral, whose neighbourhood with the ghetto shaped this topography for centuries.
