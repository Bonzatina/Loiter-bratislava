# Ramharter House — Café Regina

The Ramharter House (Ramharterov dom) with Café Regina at Hurbanovo námestie 7 is a multifunctional modernist building of 1937 designed by [[Ludwig-Christian|Christian Ludwig]]: medical offices, private flats, a café and shops under one roof, with an air-raid shelter on the second basement level. The site was a difficult one — the building is worked into the terrain of the old city moat.

![Ramharter House — Café Regina, archival photo](/assets/ramharterov-dom.jpg)

Its most expressive feature is the rounded corner lightened with extensive glazing: behind the large second-floor French windows sat the café itself. The upper floors keep a rhythm of plain windows framed by paired horizontal strips. Together with neighbouring buildings by Quastler, Steiner and Karfík, the house formed a cohesive modernist ensemble on the edge of the historic centre. From 1957 to 2000 it housed the Cyprián Majerník Gallery, an important venue for emerging artists; today the building still serves trade and services.
