# House of the Slovak League (Dom Slovenskej ligy)

The House of the Slovak League (Dom Slovenskej ligy), also known as the Liga Arcade (Liga pasáž), between Grösslingová 23 and Dunajská 18, was built in 1936 to a design by [[Harminc-Milan-Michal|Milan Michal Harminc]] — proof that the 67-year-old "nestor" was fluent in the language of modernism too. The building took the place of the former Wiesnerova street connecting the two thoroughfares, so the through-passage was preserved as a shopping arcade.

![House of the Slovak League (Dom Slovenskej ligy)](/assets/dom-slovenskej-ligy.jpg)

The complex is assembled from three masses with distinct functions: the four storeys facing Grösslingová housed the Slovak League itself, while the wing towards Dunajská — one floor higher — was given over to commerce; inside were offices, luxury six-room apartments, studios, and set-back top floors with atelier-flats and terraces. The courtyard wing held a single-storey lecture and concert hall — the League's public heart, which later operated as the "Mier" (Peace) cinema and has now been converted to retail. Today the house is residential; the ground floor holds shops and an office of Matica slovenská.
