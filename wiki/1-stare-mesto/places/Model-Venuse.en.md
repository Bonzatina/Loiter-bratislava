# Venus of the Solar System Model (Venuša)

The Venus of the [[solar-system-model|Solar System Model]] stands on May 1st Square (Námestie 1. mája) — 233 metres from the [[Fontana-Planeta-mieru|fountain-"Sun"]] on Hodžovo Square, exactly as the 1:464 million scale demands. The model is a metal pillar topped with a pointed cone, on whose tip sits the planet itself: a ball 26.1 mm across, only slightly smaller than the Earth (27.5 mm). Light from the "Sun" would take 6 minutes 1 second to get here.

![Venus of the Solar System Model (Venuša)](/assets/sustava-venusa.jpg)

The project's plaque reminds you that the planet most similar to Earth is romantic only at first glance: on Venus it rains sulphuric acid, the surface is heated to 464 °C, and the Sun sets twice in one Venusian day. The model was built with the support of the Staré Mesto borough grant scheme and co-financed by the Employment Institute; one more mini-Venus is hidden in the FunTastic toy shop.
