# Museum of Transport (Múzeum dopravy)

The Museum of Transport (Múzeum dopravy) at Šancová 1/B is the Bratislava branch of the Slovak Technical Museum (Košice), housed in the warehouse buildings of Bratislava's first steam railway station next to today's main station. The permanent exhibition is the "golden age of road and rail": ranks of veteran vehicles from bicycles and motorcycles to socialist-era limousines, and, in the open air, steam locomotives and carriages on historic tracks.
