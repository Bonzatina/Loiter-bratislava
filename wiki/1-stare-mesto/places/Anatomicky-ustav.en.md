# Institute of Anatomy (Anatomický ústav)

The Institute of Anatomy of the Faculty of Medicine, Comenius University (Anatomický ústav) at Sasinkova 2 is **the best example of [[rondocubism|Rondocubism]] in Slovakia** and the principal work of [[Silinger-Klement|Klement Šilinger]] (1924). The corner building with a semi-cylindrical entrance is composed of a compact mass segmented by semi-cylindrical pilasters under undulated cornices; inside is a monumental three-flight staircase. The function is honestly displayed on the facade: the two glazed semi-cylinders projecting onto Sasinkova hold the lecture hall and the autopsy rooms.

![The Institute of Anatomy on Sasinkova](/assets/anatomicky-ustav.jpg)

In the 1950s the medical faculty's complex of theoretical institutes was extended by Jozef Lacko and Milan Skorupa. The building serves the university to this day — future doctors still learn anatomy behind Šilinger's rounded glass.
