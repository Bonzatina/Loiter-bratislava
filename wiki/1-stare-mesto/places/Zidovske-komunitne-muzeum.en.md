# Jewish Community Museum (Židovské komunitné múzeum)

The Jewish Community Museum (Židovské komunitné múzeum) was created by Bratislava's Jewish religious community in its synagogue at Heydukova 11–13 — the city's only functioning synagogue (1923–1926, with a Cubist colonnade — a rare example of modernist Jewish sacred architecture). The core of the exhibition on the women's gallery is the Bárkány collection of Judaica: ceremonial silver, textiles, community documents, and the memory of the Bratislava rabbinate — the city of the famous [[Mauzoleum-Chatama-Sofera|Chatam Sofer]].

![The synagogue on Heydukova street](/assets/zidovske-komunitne-muzeum.jpg)

The museum is deliberately "communal": it shows the living, continuing history of Bratislava's Jews inside a working house of prayer — which is why it opens only in summer and only on days free of services.
