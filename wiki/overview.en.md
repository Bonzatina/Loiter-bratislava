# Bratislava — overview

A high-level synthesis of what is known about the city as the wiki grows. Updated whenever a new source changes the big picture.

Bratislava is the capital of Slovakia on the Danube, at the foot of the Little Carpathians, where the borders of Austria and Hungary meet. The historic Pressburg/Pozsony — coronation city of Hungarian kings — became the Slovak capital in the 20th century. The city is divided into **17 boroughs** (mestské časti) grouped into five okres (Bratislava I–V) — the boroughs are the primary axis of navigation in this wiki.
