# Church of St Nicholas in Jarovce (Kostol svätého Mikuláša)

The parish church of St Nicholas stands in the church garden beside the funeral house and is by origin **the oldest church in the whole Petržalka area**. The late baroque building was raised in 1763–1765; a tablet on the facade to the right of the entrance records the work: SUB SEBASTIANO PAUHOFF PAROCHO ANNO 1765 — "under the parish priest Sebastián Pauhoff in the year 1765".

![Church of St Nicholas in Jarovce (Kostol svätého Mikuláša)](/assets/kostol-jarovce.jpg)

The church was originally built **without a tower** — it was added only in 1884, and both dates are joined on the Latin tablet above the portal: the first part states that the church was built in 1765 under its patron Prince Esterházy, the second that "the tower is the triumph of Christ's cross" and that it was raised and furnished with new bells in 1884 by the will and at the expense of Andrej Zančič, consistorial assessor of the Holy See and parish priest, and by the will and at the expense of the local community.

The triumph proved short by historical measure: on 3 April 1945, just before the end of the war, retreating German troops blew the tower up. A proper reconstruction of the church began only in 1970, and one of the main works was a new tower — it took two years to build. Two bells were then raised into it, the great Holy Family and the small passing-bell St Andrew, which hang there still; in September 2019 two more were consecrated — the great St Nicholas and the middle Our Lady of Mariazell — and hoisted into the tower in December. In 1972–1974 the funeral house was built in the church garden and an iron fence replaced the wire one. The monument to the people of Jarovce who fell in the fighting of the First and Second World Wars was set up beside the church in 1992.
