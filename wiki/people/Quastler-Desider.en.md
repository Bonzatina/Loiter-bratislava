# Desider Quastler

Desider Quastler (1889–1944) was a Slovak architect and builder of Jewish origin, a notable figure of Bratislava's architectural scene of the 1920s–30s. A graduate of the building school in Budapest, he worked in several cities, but from 1920 chiefly in Bratislava. His work combined functionalism, purism and the theme of social housing: affordable flats for workers without loss of architectural dignity. The best surviving example is the [[Pavlacovy-dom|gallery-access house on Röntgenova]] in Petržalka (1931).

Quastler built residential and mixed-use houses: the Orion House (Dom Orion) on Hurbanovo Square, the Práca apartment house, and the multifunctional Halmos House on Suché mýto. During the Second World War he was deported for his origin to Auschwitz-Birkenau, where he perished in 1944. Many of his buildings were forgotten or attributed to others — only recent research into interwar architecture is returning his name to the city.
