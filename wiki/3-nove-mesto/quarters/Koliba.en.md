# Koliba

Koliba is a green quarter on the slopes of the Little Carpathians above the city and a favourite outing spot of Bratislavans: a place where forest, sport and family fun meet a few minutes from the centre. The name, according to historical sources, came from a popular excursion restaurant called Slamená búda ("The Straw Hut"), which locals simply called a koliba — the nickname gradually stuck to the whole locality.

![The chairlift to Kamzík](/assets/koliba-lanovka.jpg)

From a recreational fringe Koliba turned over the decades into one of the city's most desirable residential addresses — a quarter between town and forest. A chapter of its own is cinema: here stand the famous Koliba film studios, which served Slovak cinematography for decades. Above the quarter rises the [[Televizna-veza-Kamzik|Kamzík TV tower]] — the dominant and lookout of these slopes.

Today's Koliba is a concentrate of active leisure: below the TV tower, **Slovakia's first summer bobsled track** has run since 1996, the rope park has grown to 400+ metres of obstacles of every difficulty, and the Koliba bike park has become one of western Slovakia's best-known cycling grounds, with trails from family-friendly to expert (many open year-round). From here walking routes set out deeper into the Little Carpathians — to Kamzík, to [[Zelezna-studnicka|Železná studnička]] and Snežienka. Getting there is easy: a car park by the grounds, or the trolleybus to Koliba and on foot from there.
