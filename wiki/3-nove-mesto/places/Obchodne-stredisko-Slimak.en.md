# Slimák Shopping Centre (Obchodné stredisko Slimák)

"Slimák" ("snail") at Hálkova 1 is an early masterpiece by [[Matusik-Ivan|Ivan Matušík]] and one of the most charming buildings of Bratislava's [[postwar-modernism|post-war modernism]]: a two-storey shopping centre in the shape of a semi-circular segment coiled around a round atrium — hence the snail nickname. The 1957 design was built in 1960–1964 (contractor Pozemné stavby): deliveries came from the outer circumference, shoppers entered through the atrium, and a café and restaurant worked upstairs. The structure is a cast ferro-concrete dual tract with a corbel insert.

!["Slimák" shortly after completion, archival photo](/assets/slimak.jpg)

For decades the building served its neighbourhood faithfully — "architecture of the welfare state" at its best — until a 1996 renovation and enlargement disfigured the original look. The circular layout can still be read, though.
