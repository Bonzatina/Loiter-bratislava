# Hotel Academy on Mikovíniho (Hotelová akadémia Mikovíniho)

The Hotel Academy (Hotelová akadémia) on Mikovíniho street does not impress at first glance — and is nonetheless one of Bratislava's architectural treasures. Here, in the late 1950s, [[Dedecek-Vladimir|Vladimír Dedeček]] showed that a school need not be a single monumental house: it can be a considered set of pavilions, greenery and shared spaces.

![Hotel Academy on Mikovíniho (Hotelová akadémia Mikovíniho)](/assets/hotelova-akademia.jpg)

The complex — until 1979 it served as a primary school and kindergarten — is set into a slope, and its parts surround an inner courtyard: two teaching pavilions, a connecting wing with classrooms and workshops, a gymnasium and a canteen. Part of the building stands on columns, giving the pupils a roofed space beneath it — the very gesture that would later be repeated across the city. The academy's graduates include the well-known chefs Vojtech Artz and Michal Konrád.

Dedeček applied the pavilion-school principle elsewhere in Bratislava too, and out of it grew the **"Bratislava type" of school**: a set of separate pavilions for ordinary and special classrooms, a canteen and a gymnasium, arrangeable in various ways within the greenery of the school grounds. Such schools went up on Vietnamská, Nevädzová, Tokajícka, Cádrova and [[Zakladna-skola-Zaporozska|Záporožská]] streets.

## Practical Information

- Address: Mikovíniho 1
- Access: a working school, no public entry; the pavilions and courtyard can be seen from the street
