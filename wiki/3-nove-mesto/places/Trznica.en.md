# Market Hall at Trnavské mýto (Tržnica)

The city market hall (Tržnica) at Trnavské mýto (Šancová 112 / Krížna) is a late work by [[Matusik-Ivan|Ivan Matušík]] in the "machine aesthetic": designed in 1975, opened in 1983. The city's [[Stara-trznica|Old Market Hall]] had lost its function after the war — for decades it housed the studios of Slovak Television — and the State Trade Design Institute (ŠPUO) built a modern "commercial container" to replace it: a solid ferroconcrete skeleton, a glass curtain wall, grand staircases at both ends.

![The Market Hall shortly after opening, archival photo](/assets/trznica.jpg)

Inside are two levels of market rows with stands for private vendors of produce and goods; a large supermarket worked in the cellar. The Tržnica is alive today: a market on weekdays, and at weekends concerts, flea markets and city festivals that have made the building loved anew.
