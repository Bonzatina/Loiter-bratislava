# Slovak Olympic and Sports Museum (Slovenské olympijské a športové múzeum)

The Slovak Olympic and Sports Museum (Slovenské olympijské a športové múzeum) is the museum of the Slovak Olympic Committee in the House of Sport (Dom športu) at Olympijské námestie 1, in the Pasienky sports quarter among stadiums and halls. The collection documents the country's sporting glory: Olympic medals and gear of Slovak athletes, Olympic torch relay torches, trophies and archives of sports societies from Sokol to the present day.
