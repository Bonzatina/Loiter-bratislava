# Museum of Trade (Múzeum obchodu)

The Museum of Trade (Múzeum obchodu) in the Juraj Albert curia at Linzbothova 16 is Slovakia's only museum of the history of commerce and innkeeping. Founded in 1983 as a documentation centre of the Ministry of Trade and Tourism of the Slovak Socialist Republic, it has assembled a unique collection: interiors of old shops with counters, scales and cash registers, tin and enamel advertising signs, century-old packaging and bottles — a whole encyclopaedia of what commerce looked like from the monarchy to socialism. The museum runs an inn with its own museum brewery.
