# Rudavskys Magic Garden

Rudavskys Magic Garden at Korytnická 37 in [[2-podunajske-biskupice|Podunajské Biskupice]] is a unique art garden, museum and living open-air gallery: on a relatively small plot, among sixty-year-old maples, birches, pines, lindens and grapevines, stand and hang **more than 300 artworks** — sculptures, objects, paintings and textiles.

![Rudavskys Magic Garden](/assets/rudavskys-magic-garden.jpg)

The place arose in the 1960s from the talent and passion of the sculptor Andrej Rudavský and the artist Mária Rudavská — among the brightest figures of the fine art of their generation. The sculpture garden on the city's edge was a meeting place for legends of the Galandovci circle and people of other genres — actors, directors, writers, musicians; despite the displeasure of the communist regime the garden kept growing and never lost its genius loci. Today it is compared to the world's famous art spaces — from the Tarot Garden in Tuscany and Park Güell to Little Sparta and Lithuania's Hill of Witches. The parents' work is continued by their son, the internationally renowned multimedia artist Ondrej Rudavský, whose return to Slovakia gave the garden a new impulse; the back of the grounds holds the "second generation gallery" with his works and the signature jewellery of Jacqueline Rudavsky.
