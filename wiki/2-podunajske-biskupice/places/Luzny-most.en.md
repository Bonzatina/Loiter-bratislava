# Lužný Bridge (Lužný most)

Lužný Bridge (Lužný most) is the youngest and southernmost Danube bridge of Bratislava, opened in 2021 on the D4/R7 bypass. It takes its name from the floodplain (lužné) forests among which it steps across the Danube far from the built-up city — between the [[5-petrzalka|Petržalka]] bank near Jarovce and [[2-podunajske-biskupice|Podunajské Biskupice]] by the Slovnaft refinery.

![Lužný Bridge (Lužný most)](/assets/luzny-most.jpg)

Strictly speaking it is a whole chain of bridges (súmostie), built in 2018–2021: the eastern approach over the Biskupice river arm, the Lužný Bridge proper over the Danube — a three-span single-box concrete beam with a 170-metre main span — a bridge over the rowing course and the western approach over the Jarovce arm. Together they closed the southern arc of the city bypass; for pedestrians and cyclists the bridge became a new way into the floodplain forests and to the Danube islands.
