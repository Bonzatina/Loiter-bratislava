# UFO Sculpture

The UFO is a metal flying saucer landed on a mound in the middle of the park of the Medzi jarkami estate, on the boundary of [[2-podunajske-biskupice|Podunajské Biskupice]] and [[2-vrakuna|Vrakuňa]]. The sculpture by the academic sculptor Juraj Hovorka has stood here since 1977 — one of the icons of Slovak park art and the recognisable landmark of the whole neighbourhood: a disc 8 metres in diameter hovering 4.6 metres above its concrete base, among the paneláky of Bieloruská street, not far from the famous [[Pentagon]] block.

![UFO Sculpture](/assets/ufo-socha.jpg)

A child of the era's fascination with spaceflight, the saucer became a place of games and alien stories for generations of the estate's children. It has film fame too: it appeared in the Czechoslovak science-fiction comedy "Warm Greetings from the Globe" (Srdečný pozdrav ze zeměkoule, 1982) — in which, incidentally, alien "A" was played by [[Lasica-Milan|Milan Lasica]] — and in the same year served as a set for the TV series "The Magic Little Town" (Kúzelné mestečko); it is through the screen that audiences far beyond the estate know it.

The quarter's reputation nearly destroyed the object: the Pentagon's surroundings were long notorious for drug users and the homeless, in the 1990s sheets of the aluminium cladding kept vanishing from the structure, and removal of the saucer was seriously discussed on safety grounds — but the estate's residents stood up for it, the UFO being part of their childhood and of the place's identity. In 2014 the Podunajské Biskupice borough restored the sculpture — with the same materials and techniques as the original: the missing parts were replaced, the surface renewed, the surroundings put in order (the old concrete stairs leading up the mound were removed). Recognition followed: the UFO was added to Bratislava's city-wide register of local heritage (zoznam pamätihodností — over 600 entries, maintained by the City Institute for Monument Protection) — in the same update package as Petržalka's sundial and the wartime air-raid-shelter arrow markings.
