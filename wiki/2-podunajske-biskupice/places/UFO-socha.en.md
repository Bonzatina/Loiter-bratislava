# UFO Sculpture

The UFO is a metal flying saucer landed on a mound in the middle of the park of the Medzi jarkami estate, on the boundary of [[2-podunajske-biskupice|Podunajské Biskupice]] and [[2-vrakuna|Vrakuňa]]. The 1970s sculpture by the academic sculptor Juraj Hovorka is one of the icons of Slovak park art of that era and the recognisable landmark of the whole neighbourhood: a disc 8 metres in diameter hovering 4.6 metres above its concrete base, among the paneláky of Bieloruská street, not far from the famous [[Pentagon]] block.

![UFO Sculpture](/assets/ufo-socha.jpg)

The saucer has film fame too: it appeared in the Czechoslovak science-fiction comedy "Warm Greetings from the Globe" (Srdečný pozdrav ze zeměkoule, 1982) — in which, incidentally, alien "A" was played by [[Lasica-Milan|Milan Lasica]] — and it is through the film that audiences far beyond the estate know it.

The quarter's reputation nearly destroyed the object: the Pentagon's surroundings were long notorious for drug users and the homeless, "dubious individuals" gradually dismantled the rusting structure, and the saucer and its surroundings grew ever more dangerous. In 2014 the Podunajské Biskupice borough restored the sculpture: the missing parts were replaced, the body polished, and the old concrete stairs leading up the mound removed. Recognition followed: the UFO was added to Bratislava's city-wide register of local heritage (zoznam pamätihodností — over 600 entries, maintained by the City Institute for Monument Protection) — in the same update package as Petržalka's sundial and the wartime air-raid-shelter arrow markings.
