# Dúbravka Museum (Dúbravské múzeum)

The Dúbravka Museum (Dúbravské múzeum) at Pod záhradami 39 is a small local-history museum of the former village, housed in the renovated house of the parish priest (opened in 2011). It all began with a collection of folk culture objects shown in 2006 at the local library; some items returned from the Bratislava City Museum, and the collection took on a life of its own.

![The Dúbravka Museum — the former priest's house](/assets/dubravske-muzeum.jpg)

Five thematic rooms lead from oldest and medieval Dúbravka through the history of the axe in Slovakia and old photographs of the district to viticultural equipment — the village's principal craft; there is also a digital archive with chronicles, cadastral maps and the urbarium of 1926–1938. The exhibition recreates a farmer's room with traditional furniture, kitchen utensils, a sleeping corner, folk clothes and children's toys.
