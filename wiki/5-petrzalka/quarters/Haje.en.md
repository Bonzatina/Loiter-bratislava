# Háje

Háje is the middle part of the Petržalka estate and the phase with which panel construction began: Háje 1–3 plus the Economic University locality. Older names lie inside the quarter too — [[Ovsiste|Ovsište]] with Staré Ovsište, [[Kupalisko-Lido|Lido]] and [[Elyzium|Elýzium]] by the Danube bank, and Starý háj with its [[Kaplnka-v-Starom-haji|chapel]].

The [[Chorvatske-rameno|Croatian Arm]] runs through the quarter, and the tram now runs beside it — along the line of the metro that was planned for the linear centre and never built. Among its public buildings is the Zrkadlový háj culture house, which also gave its name to the neighbouring forest park.
