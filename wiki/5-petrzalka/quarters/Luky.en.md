# Lúky

Lúky is the southern and most extensive part of the Petržalka estate: nine phases of Lúky plus Stredné lúky, with the Draždiak lake locality along its edge. It was built second, between Háje and Dvory.

Here the estate ends and water and open ground begin: [[Velky-Drazdiak|Veľký Draždiak]] lies at its eastern edge, the [[Chorvatske-rameno|Croatian Arm]] runs along the quarter, and beyond it the Danube dyke, which you can reach by bicycle from anywhere in Lúky. The Lúky culture house is one of those district cultural venues found in practically every part of Petržalka.
