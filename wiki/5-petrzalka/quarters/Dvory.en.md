# Dvory

Dvory is the northern of the three great phases of the Petržalka housing estate: Dvory 1–6, and with them Old Petržalka and the factory locality of [[Matadorka|Matador]]. Panel construction began earliest here — in the phases Háje → Lúky → Dvory — with the old village demolished last, 99 per cent of its buildings going under the panels.

Hence the quarter's peculiarity: beside the standard blocks, surviving streets of old Petržalka with their family houses hold on — the little that remains of the largest village in pre-war Czechoslovakia. The Matadorka factory site adjoins Dvory to the west, [[Sad-Janka-Krala|Janko Kráľ Garden]] and the Danube bank to the east.
