# Ovsište

Ovsište is an old name on the eastern edge of [[Haje|Háje]], by the Danube bank: first an outlying settlement of pre-war Petržalka, then a piece of the panel estate that kept the name, and with it Staré Ovsište.

The place has a cheerless biography. Ovsište belonged to the belt of poor colonies that ringed the swollen interwar village: even after the Second World War, more than 250 of its 340 houses were shacks. Almost nothing of that Petržalka survives — the panel construction of the 1970s swept the whole right bank — but the name held on, as [[Elyzium|Elýzium]] and the [[Psia-kolonia|Dog Colony]] held on in memory and on the map. Today the quarter's chief landmarks are the Ovsište culture house and the [[Obchodny-dom-Ovsiste|department store]], the former "little Prior".
