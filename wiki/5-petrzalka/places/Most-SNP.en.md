# SNP Bridge and the UFO (Most SNP)

The Bridge of the Slovak National Uprising (Most SNP) is Bratislava's calling card and its most contested symbol: a cable-stayed bridge without a single pier in the Danube's channel, hung from a slanting 85-metre pylon on the right bank, atop which floats the lens-shaped "UFO" restaurant with an observation deck and the best panorama of the city. The design by architects Jozef Lacko, Ladislav Kušnír and Ivan Slameň with engineer Arpád Tesár won the competition of 1964–1967; traffic opened in 1972.

![The SNP Bridge with the UFO and panelák Petržalka beyond](/assets/most-snp.jpg)

The daring of the structure is inseparable from a trauma: to make way for the motorway to the bridge, almost all of Podhradie was demolished — legendary [[Vydrica]] and the old Jewish quarter by St Martin's Cathedral (the [[Muzeum-zidovskej-kultury|Museum of Jewish Culture]] on the surviving Židovská street keeps its memory). The urban decision is cursed to this day, but the bridge itself has long been recognised as an outstanding work of engineering and [[postwar-modernism|late modernism]] — and the city's great "alien" silhouette.

## Practical Information

The UFO observation deck is open daily (the ticket counts towards the restaurant bill); entry from the Petržalka side of the bridge. The pedestrian and cycling level runs under the roadway.
