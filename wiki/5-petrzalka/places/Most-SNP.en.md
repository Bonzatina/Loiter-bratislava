# SNP Bridge and the UFO (Most SNP)

The Bridge of the Slovak National Uprising (Most SNP) is Bratislava's calling card and its most contested symbol: a cable-stayed bridge without a single pier in the Danube's channel, hung from a slanting pylon on the right bank, whose top — at a height of 95 metres — is crowned by the lens-shaped "UFO" restaurant with an observation deck and the best panorama of the city. The design by architects Jozef Lacko, Ladislav Kušnír and Ivan Slameň with engineer [[Tesar-Arpad|Arpád Tesár]] won the competition of 1964–1967; traffic opened in 1972. The bridge, 431 metres long and 21 wide, was the first in Slovakia and among the first in the world without piers in the channel; the design inspired a "twin" in Riga. Construction began in 1967 (final completion came in 1974), and instead of the riveting proposed by a foreign firm a welded structure was chosen — a world rarity at the time. After 1989 the bridge was renamed the New Bridge (Nový most), but in 2012, for the fortieth anniversary of its opening, the historic name was returned; in 2001 it was proclaimed Slovakia's "construction of the century", and since 2018 it has been a national cultural monument.

![The SNP Bridge with the UFO and panelák Petržalka beyond](/assets/most-snp.jpg)

The daring of the structure is inseparable from a trauma: to make way for the motorway to the bridge, almost all of Podhradie was demolished — legendary [[Vydrica]] and the old Jewish quarter by [[Dom-svateho-Martina|St Martin's Cathedral]], with the synagogue and Rybné námestie (the [[Muzeum-zidovskej-kultury|Museum of Jewish Culture]] on the surviving Židovská street keeps its memory). The urban decision is cursed to this day, but the bridge itself has long been recognised as an outstanding work of engineering and [[postwar-modernism|late modernism]] — and the city's great "alien" silhouette. For its characteristic shape the UFO tower was admitted to the World Federation of Great Towers; world celebrities from Brian May and Pamela Anderson to Jay-Z have visited its deck.

## Practical Information

The UFO observation deck is open daily (the ticket counts towards the restaurant bill); entry from the Petržalka side of the bridge. A ten-person lift in the pylon leg takes you up, the emergency staircase counts 430 steps; the restaurant and bar sit at the 85-metre mark, and in clear weather the terrace view reaches some hundred kilometres — from the Danube to the Little Carpathians. The pedestrian and cycling level runs under the roadway.
