# Memorial to the Victims of Extremism (Pamätník obetiam extrémizmu)

The Memorial to the Victims of Extremism (Pamätník obetiam extrémizmu) on Tyršovo nábrežie is a quiet place of grief on the Danube's right bank where candles are lit every November: **on 4 November 2005 the student Daniel Tupý was murdered here**, becoming Slovakia's symbol of the victims of far-right violence. The memorial's illuminated metal arm reaching skyward is visible along the embankment from afar at dusk.

![Memorial to the Victims of Extremism (Pamätník obetiam extrémizmu)](/assets/pamatnik-obetiam-extremizmu.jpg)

For the fifteenth anniversary of the murder, the city and the Metropolitan Institute of Bratislava presented a project to revitalise the memorial's surroundings — to return the place its solemn character: reduce parking, replace paved areas with lawns, plant trees, add walking routes, lighting and street furniture, and emphasise the site's significance with a gentle wave of terrain around the central space. The design is deliberately modest — not to compete with the memorial, but to support its presence in the context of the embankment.
