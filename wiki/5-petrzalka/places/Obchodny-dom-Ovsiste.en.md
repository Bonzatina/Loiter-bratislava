# The Ovsište Shopping Centre — the "Little Prior" (OD Centrum / Bratislavský)

The Centrum shopping and cultural house (OD Centrum) with its clock tower on Ovsištské Square — then N. K. Krupskaya Square — was built as a showcase of the "fulfilment of the electoral programme" of the new Ovsište estate: first came groceries, a tobacconist, a bookshop and a drugstore, a shoe-repair shop and a post office, and by the grand opening in December 1985 a delicatessen, a confectionery, a restaurant, hairdressers and a house of culture had been added. Locals called the complex the "little Prior" — after the big department store on Kamenné Square.

![The Centrum shopping centre in Ovsište, 1982](/assets/obchodny-dom-ovsiste.jpg)

Its post-socialist fate was harsh: within a decade of the opening the decaying complex with its fouled corners was already nicknamed the "House of Horror" — as co-owner Juraj Bratislavský admitted (his family bought the building in 1997), "even the racketeers were demotivated to visit us". A second life came in the 2020s: after buying out his partners' shares, Bratislavský renamed the centre "Bratislavský" — simply after his own surname — renovated it, and the tower clock, which had worked only briefly after its 1980s launch, ticks and chimes again — thanks in part to a collection by the readers of the Petržalka News.
