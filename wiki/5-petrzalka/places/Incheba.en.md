# Incheba Exhibition Grounds (Výstavisko Incheba)

Incheba Expo is Bratislava's largest exhibition complex, standing on the right bank of the Danube just beyond the [[Most-SNP|SNP Bridge]]. The name is an abbreviation of INternational CHEmical BratislavA: the complex was conceived as the home of international chemical fairs. The project was created by [[Dedecek-Vladimir|Vladimír Dedeček]] — the administrative building appears in his list of works under 1989, the exhibition grounds themselves under 1990.

![Incheba Exhibition Grounds (Výstavisko Incheba)](/assets/incheba.jpg)

The original idea was far larger than what stands today: the complex was to become the core of a new city centre, with extensive development, many buildings and its own port on the Danube. Its financial weight meant the project was never realised in full; it was later reworked and built in reduced form — a fate that links Incheba to [[5-petrzalka|Petržalka]]'s unbuilt linear centre and the metro that never came.

The site lies on Einsteinova street, hemmed in by traffic on every side: to the south the motorway to the Austrian border crossing and the Port Bridge in the middle of Einsteinova, to the east the SNP Bridge and Panónska cesta, to the north and west Viedenská cesta. Inside are several large multifunctional halls, ground-level parking and parking on the first floor beside one of the halls, a high-rise and the Incheba hotel. Today it hosts fairs such as Coneco, tennis tournaments, concerts and exhibitions — so the complex is alive, if not for the purpose it was built for.
