# Bilkovič's "Object" (Objekt)

The "Object" (Objekt) by Alexander Bilkovič (1990) is an abstract sculpture of artificial stone, 2.5 metres tall, organically fused with the facade of the health centre at Fedinova 9 (Zdravotné stredisko Fedinova). It is not a classic relief: the streamlined, "vegetative" or anthropomorphic form changes with the angle of view and leaves the reading to the viewer. For healthcare institutions of the era this was a rarity — they preferred easily legible figurative subjects on the theme of "the people's health", yet at the entrance to this Petržalka clinic stands an almost surreal object.

![Bilkovič's "Object" on Fedinova (Objekt)](/assets/objekt-bilkovica.jpg)

Alexander Bilkovič was a notable Slovak designer and sculptor; his works in public space are recognized by monumental form, dynamic lines and smooth surfaces — whether free sculpture or functional elements. The original lettering has disappeared from the "Object" over time; otherwise it survives intact.
