# Veľký Draždiak

Veľký Draždiak is Petržalka's lake and natural bathing place: thirteen hectares of water between the [[Chorvatske-rameno|Croatian Arm]] and the Hospital of Saints Cyril and Methodius on Antolská street. In summer people swim here, ride pedal boats and kayaks and sunbathe; a running track has circled the lake since 2013, and cyclists pass along the shore on their way to the dyke, easily reached from anywhere in the district along the arm. Besides swimmers, large flocks of swans, ducks and other waterfowl keep to the water.

![Veľký Draždiak](/assets/velky-drazdiak.jpg)

The lake in its present form appeared around 1982, after gravel extraction and the deepening of an earlier stretch of water that old Petržalka residents used to call "Šutrovka". There are a few snack bars by the water, pedal boats for hire and beach volleyball courts; beside the Oáza restaurant there is a sauna, and in winter you may meet the "Petržalka polar bears", devotees of cold-water swimming. To the south stands the four-star Bonbón hotel, to the west Oáza, a clinic and a tennis school, to the east the Antolská hospital and housing blocks, to the north the Zrkadlový háj forest park.

One detail explains why this is not an official bathing place and why there are no lifeguards: a very-high-voltage power line (110 kV) runs directly above the lake. Swimming is at your own risk, which in hot weather stops almost nobody; construction rubbish and concrete blocks remain in the water and can injure, and dogs are bathed despite the ban. Petržalka residents do organise regular clean-ups, though, and the litter situation is improving. A children's playground was built by the lake in 2006.
