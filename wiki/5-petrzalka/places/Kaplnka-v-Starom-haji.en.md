# Chapel of the Holy Cross in Starý háj (Kaplnka sv. Kríža v Starom háji)

The Chapel of the Holy Cross in Starý háj (Kaplnka sv. Kríža v Starom háji) is a small church in the floodplain forest in the south-east of [[5-petrzalka|Petržalka]], one of the rare monuments of pre-panelák, "village" Petržalka. Its history began with the elements: the ice flood of 1809 washed a wooden cross into Starý háj. Where it had floated from was never discovered, so the cross was raised on the very spot where it was found.

![Chapel in Starý háj (Kaplnka v Starom háji)](/assets/kaplnka-stary-haj.jpg)

The found cross soon decayed, and in 1844 members of the shooting society replaced it with a new wooden crucifix, consecrated by the priest Kremlicska. In the 1860s a wooden bell tower was erected nearby with a 40-kilogram bell cast by Anton Belloni, and in 1869 a new stone cross in an iron enclosure was raised with 920 gold coins collected by subscription — it was consecrated in June of that year by Carl Heiller, the Pressburg city parish priest.

For the centenary of the cross's discovery a chapel rose on the spot: the building with a small wooden bell frame was carried out by the firm of builder Ludwig Gratzl, the stone cross of 1869 was moved inside, and on 26 September 1909 the church was consecrated by the priest Edmund Zandt. The interior preserves a two-part plaque in German and Hungarian with the names of the building committee members and donors — among them recognizable Pressburg families (the Angermayers, Rumpelmayer, the shooting society, the city of Pozsony).
