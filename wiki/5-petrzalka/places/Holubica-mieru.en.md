# Dove of Peace (Holubica mieru)

The Dove of Peace (Holubica mieru) by Karol Lacko (1938–2007) is an 18-metre steel pylon whose top bends into the silhouette of a stylized bird in flight. Petržalka's most monumental "peace" sign was erected in 1981–1984 by Dolnozemská cesta, at what was then the southern entrance to Bratislava: the colossal dove greeted those arriving from Hungary — a Cold-War-era gesture of the "city of peace". Like the other works of the series, it also served as a landmark among the identical panel blocks of the Lúky quarter.

![The Dove of Peace by Dolnozemská cesta](/assets/holubica-mieru.jpg)

After 1989 the sculpture rusted for decades while its fate was debated — from demolition to relocation; in 2021 Petržalka finally restored the dove comprehensively, returned its anticorrosive whiteness and added lighting. In the spring of 2022 it was lit in the colours of the Ukrainian flag — the old symbol of peace speaking again. Today it is a protected landmark of the district, next to the Economic University campus.
