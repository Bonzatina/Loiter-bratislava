# Matadorka — the Matador Works (Areál Matador)

Matadorka is the factory site on the western edge of [[Dvory|Dvory]] with which Petržalka's industrial growth began. The firm was founded in 1905 as "Matador — Gummi und Balata Werke" (its name until 1911) and made chiefly rubber hoses and drive belts. From 1925 it produced tyres — the first maker in what was then Czechoslovakia — and in the thirties "matadorky" were fitted to all the famous Czechoslovak cars, working for Škoda, Tatra, Aero and others. From a small works with eighty employees the site grew into a giant with two thousand workers by the 1980s.

The firm was nationalised in 1946, and a year later a new national enterprise was split off from the old Bratislava company in Púchov — the Matador that today belongs to Continental. After 1989 production moved out of Petržalka and parts of the site were sold off to private investors.

The place is now living its third life. The industrial zone is turning into a mixed urban quarter: the Nová Matadorka project — five buildings of no more than eight storeys, 203 apartments and 25 serviced ones, retail and offices, 468 underground parking spaces — keeps parts of the original halls with small piazzas between them. The site's central building, the enamel works (smaltovňa), is described as an exceptionally interesting industrial object, though it holds no official heritage protection; the mill building opposite it is slated for reconstruction. The project obtained its territorial decision in September 2021.
