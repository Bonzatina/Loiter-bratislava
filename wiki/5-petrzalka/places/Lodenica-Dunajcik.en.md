# The Dunajčík Boathouse (Lodenica Dunajčík)

Dunajčík is Slovakia's oldest paddling club with an unbroken history: in 1924 ten Petržalka boys founded a water-scout troop and named it after the river that had captivated them. Over a hundred years the club has raised hundreds of paddlers and become one of the pillars of canoeing — Slovakia's most successful Olympic sport; boathouse couples here often became married ones.

![The Dunajčík boathouse on Klokočova](/assets/lodenica-dunajcik.jpg)

The boathouse's biography is almost a history of the century. The Dunajčíks built their first boathouse with their own hands in the early 1930s at the mouth of the Ovsište branch (the Lido bay near today's Apollo bridge); at the start of the war, after the Reich annexed Petržalka, they dismantled it and moved it to the Winter Harbour — where the second boathouse, together with the boats, was destroyed in the bombing of the Apollo refinery. In 1946 the club returned to the right bank: decommissioned military sheds from Železná studienka were moved to Klokočova road "temporarily" — and that temporary solution serves to this day, together with the wooden furniture from a government bunker that sets the tone of the clubroom. The boathouse was flooded in the historic high waters of 2013 and again in September 2024, but each time the club rebuilds, and it is preparing a project for a new flood-resilient boathouse. Dunajčík also means public traditions: the Danube Marathon on the old riverbed between Čunovo and Sap, "Paddling Without Barriers" for children with disabilities, and the spring clean-up of Bratislava's riverbanks.
