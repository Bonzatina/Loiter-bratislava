# Message of Peace (Posolstvo mieru)

*Message of Peace* (Posolstvo mieru) by sculptor Pavol Tóth (1982) is a bronze figure of a woman with a dove in a courtyard on Budatínska street — the most lyrical of panelák Petržalka's "peace" sculptures. Installed in a landscaped courtyard for 390,000 crowns (1983), it is one of those works that helped residents find their way among identical panel blocks — while carrying the era's obligatory theme of peace.

![Pavol Tóth's *Message of Peace* in the Budatínska courtyard](/assets/posolstvo-mieru.jpg)

The sculpture has survived facade insulation and the change of eras — and still greets the courtyard's residents. The peace theme was near-mandatory in 1980s Petržalka: it is also carried by the [[Holubica-mieru|Dove of Peace]] at the city's southern gateway and the [[Mural-Mier|"Peace" mural]] on Osuského.
