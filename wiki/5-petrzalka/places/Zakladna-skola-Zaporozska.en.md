# Pavilion School on Záporožská (ZŠ Záporožská)

The primary school at Záporožská 8 in Petržalka is an example of [[Dedecek-Vladimir|Vladimír Dedeček]]'s 1960s school architecture, the so-called "Bratislava type". The 24-class school is assembled from five linked structures: two three-storey main teaching pavilions with deep transverse classrooms arranged along a vertical circulation core (the classrooms get cross-lighting), a two-storey administrative block with the school kitchen, a two-storey pavilion for specialised classrooms, and a gym. An open pergola corridor links the pavilions.

![Pavilion school on Záporožská (ZŠ Záporožská), archival photo](/assets/zs-zaporozska.jpg)

The construction is modest — masonry walls with prefabricated ceiling slabs — but the planning idea was advanced for its time: a school as a little town of pavilions among greenery rather than a corridor "barracks". The building predates panelák Petržalka and outlived it: the school still operates in its original role.
