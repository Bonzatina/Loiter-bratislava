# The Fairy-tale Tree (Rozprávkový strom)

The Fairy-tale Tree (Rozprávkový strom) by Erna Masarovičová (1979) grows in the atrium of the children's policlinic of Karlova Ves (Líščie údolie 57). From star-shaped roots on a round concrete base rises a slender iron trunk, and the branching crown is woven of bent and perforated biomorphic forms: human figures and stylized heads, animals, plant tendrils and leaves. The round eyes of the faces are made of blue enamel and translucent vitreous glaze; among the branches stands out a little rooster with a tall comb and a beak curved into a smile, and accents of red glimmer here and there. The sculpture, about 2.1 metres tall, is composed in the round — with every step the perforations and bends reveal new see-throughs and details. An enamel medallion carries the signature EM 1980.

![The Fairy-tale Tree by Erna Masarovičová (Rozprávkový strom)](/assets/rozpravkovy-strom.jpg)

The tree has had its drama: during careless handling in atrium works its dominant feature vanished — the sun with two faces and blue eyes. After the researchers of the Art of the City project intervened, the piece was found, and the work underwent professional restoration under the supervision of Katarína Kissoczy, the artist's daughter and keeper of her estate. Its fate is nonetheless a sad one: after the policlinic's reconstruction the greenery was removed from the atrium, the tree was pushed into a corner on grey paving, and the glass doors to the atrium are blocked by chairs in the corridor — its main audience, the little patients for whom the tree was created, sees it only through the glass.
