# Lafranconi Bridge (Most Lafranconi)

Lafranconi Bridge (Most Lafranconi, the spelling Lanfranconi also occurs) is the westernmost of the city's Danube bridges, Bratislava's concrete gateway from the direction of Austria and Czechia. In the project it was called the Youth Bridge (Most mládeže), but after 1989 it took the name of the [[Internat-Lafranconi|Lafranconi student dormitory]] on the left bank — and through it the memory of engineer [[Lanfranconi-Enea|Grazioso Enea Lanfranconi]], the regulator of the Danube. Construction began in 1985 and traffic opened in 1991 (1992 by other accounts).

![Lafranconi Bridge (Most Lafranconi)](/assets/most-lafranconi.jpg)

Structurally these are two identical, mutually independent parallel bridges of prestressed concrete on a shared substructure: a continuous seven-span beam with a 174-metre main span, fixed into a pier in the middle of the river. The upper level carries the D2 motorway towards Prague and Brno — 761 metres long and 30 wide, four lanes — while beneath the roadway hide pedestrian and cycling paths and a gas pipeline; the bridge carries about 44 thousand vehicles a day. For cyclists it is the usual way from the centre to [[5-petrzalka|Petržalka]], the Pečniansky forest and the Danube cycle path towards Vienna.
