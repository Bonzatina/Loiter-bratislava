# The "Vent" (Výduch)

The "Vent" (Výduch) is an elegant ventilation column by the southern terrace between the Bratislava 4 post office and the children's policlinic of Karlova Ves (Líščie údolie 57), probably the work of architect Albert Ščepán (1975–1980). An object utilitarian in purpose — an air-handling outlet — received surprisingly careful finishing: the nine-metre column of square cross-section is clad in stone slabs with vertical fluting, divided horizontally into seven geometric fields, resting on a concrete pedestal below and crowned by a slightly overhanging stone slab above.

![The "Vent" by the Karlova Ves policlinic (Výduch)](/assets/vyduch-liscie-udolie.jpg)

Such small technical objects, treated seriously by their architects, can be found by large public buildings all over Bratislava — sometimes reading as free-standing sculpture, sometimes as a witty piece of design. The "Vent" still works to this day; the researchers of the Art of the City project note that clearing the neglected nook by the car park under the terrace would let the column shine as an ornament of public space.
