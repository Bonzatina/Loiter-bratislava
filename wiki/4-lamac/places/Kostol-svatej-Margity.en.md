# Church of St Margaret (Kostol svätej Margity)

The Roman Catholic parish church of St Margaret of Antioch (Kostol svätej Margity) on Vrančovičova street in Lamač was built in 1947–1951 — one of the last works of the 80-year-old [[Harminc-Milan-Michal|Milan Michal Harminc]] — and consecrated on 1 July 1951. It replaced an old 16th-century church on the site that had long been too small for the parish.

![Church of St Margaret (Kostol svätej Margity)](/assets/kostol-svatej-margity.jpg)

The three-nave volume reads on the outside through the stepped difference in heights; the facades are flat, articulated by a horizontal strip and subtly raised windows, while all the decoration is concentrated at the entrance: a stone-framed portal, a mosaic of St Margaret above the large window, figures of saints at the sides, and a rectangular side tower with a clock under a pyramidal roof. Since 1996 the church has been a national cultural monument of Slovakia; services continue to this day.
