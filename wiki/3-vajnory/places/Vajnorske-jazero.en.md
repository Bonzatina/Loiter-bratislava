# Vajnory Lake (Vajnorské jazero)

The Vajnory lakes lie on the south-eastern edge of [[3-vajnory|Vajnory]], by the road out to Senec — pit lakes that locals know as "Bager", after the excavator that dug them. This is one of the borough's two significant bodies of water: the other is the chain of ponds at Sprinzl's farm in the north-western, Little Carpathian half of the cadastre.

![Vajnory Lake (Vajnorské jazero)](/assets/vajnorske-jazero.jpg)

The place counts as a regional biocentre — together with the Vajnory valley, Sprinzl's farm, the Rača canal and the Struha stream. The reason is the water: Vajnory is marked by a very high groundwater table, fed from the Little Carpathians and flowing north-west to south-east, its level also affected by backwater from the Danube. The main hydrological network belongs to the basin of the Šúr canal, dug during the Second World War, fed by the Račí and Račiansky streams, the Vajnorský stream, the Kratina and the Struha.
